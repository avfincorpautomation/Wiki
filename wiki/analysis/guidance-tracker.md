## Track record so far

Of the dated promises from January 2026 that have fallen due (Q4 FY26 and FY26), **all three numeric ones were missed**: Q4 volume, FY26 volume and FY26 EBITDA. FY27 guidance was restated lower four months later. This had happened before. In Q1 FY26 management "prudently moderated our full-year volume guidance" (AR p.51), to 10–15% (Q3C p.3). In January the CMD said those earlier cuts "hurt badly" and that "we are not ready to take back the guidelines at any cost" (Q3C p.9).

**Management's explanation (4 May 2026):** the company was on track "till 28th of February". Then the Middle East war brought steel shortages, Dubai at 40% utilisation, channel destocking, gas shortages and labour issues. "If war had not started, we would have met our guidance" (Q4C p.3–4, p.10). On the same call the January guidance was recalled as ~1 Mn ton in Q4 at "INR5,300-INR5,500 per ton" (Q4C p.10). The January transcript says ₹5,500 "minimum". FY27 guidance of 15–20% was reaffirmed, but the CMD added "20% is difficult … targeting 15%" (Q4C p.8).

**Q2 FY27 check (October 2026):**
- **Margin beat:** EBITDA/ton ₹5,999, above the ₹5,000–5,500 range given in August (Q2P p.7; Q1C p.4).
- **EBITDA on track:** H1 EBITDA +21% (Q2P p.7–8).
- **Q2 volume close:** 963 thousand ton against ~10 lakh (−4%) (Q2P p.13; Q1C p.18).
- **Volume guidance withdrawn in effect:** company-wide FY27 volume guidance was replaced by a commitment for the APL Apollo brand and Roofing only (Q2C 06:31).
- **Balance sheet went the wrong way:** gross debt doubled to ≈₹918 cr and finance cost rose 35% QoQ, against the promises to retire the ₹500 cr liability and cut interest to "almost zero" (Q2R p.9–11).
- **Restructuring delivered:** the Blue Ocean sale and the APL Apollo Mart liquidation are complete (Q2R p.12, p.18).
- **Pattern so far:** the volume promises keep slipping and are re-framed, while EBITDA/ton is the metric management now emphasises and is delivering.

**Q1 FY27 check (August 2026):** three promises fell due in Q1, and **two were missed**:
- **Volume missed:** 7.45 lakh ton against the ~8.75 lakh target, −15% (Q1P p.12).
- **Interest cost missed:** it rose to ₹389 Mn, +23% QoQ, against "almost zero" (Q1P p.19).
- **Margin met:** EBITDA/ton ₹5,522, inside the ₹5,000–5,500 range (Q1P p.7).

The ₹500 cr liability retirement (due by Q2) shows no visible progress. The capacity timeline is now "two and a half years" from the CSO, which points to FY29, while the deck keeps "By FY28" (Q1C p.4; Q1P p.10). FY27 guidance was again reaffirmed. It now rests on a back-ended path: Q2 10, Q3 10.5 and Q4 12 lakh ton (Q1C p.18).
