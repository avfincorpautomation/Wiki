# Open questions — Apl Apollo Tubes Ltd

Unresolved questions, data gaps and contradictions between sources.

## Questions

1. **Operating cash flow definition (minor).** Management's headline OCF of ₹20.0 Bn (₹2,002 cr) is ~₹101 cr below the audited ₹2,103.19 cr (S p.26, p.345); FY25 shows a similar small gap (Q3P p.20). What is the definition? Low priority. → [[financials]] *Gap widened in H1 FY27:* presentation OCF ₹6,827 Mn (₹683 cr) vs statutory ₹593.89 cr, a ~₹89 cr (13%) gap (Q2P p.20; Q2R p.11). See C28. Priority raised.
2. **Supplier finance.** Is the ₹1,381.96 cr of supplier-financed payables (S p.386) the "₹500 crore current liability" the Chairman says will be retired in FY27 (S p.21)? What would net cash be on a stricter definition? → [[supplier-finance]] *New clue:* in February 2026 ICRA recorded non-fund-based limits falling from ₹1,000 cr to nil, and cash credit rising from ₹1,460 cr to ₹2,975 cr (ICRA p.1). That fits acceptances or LCs being replaced by fund-based borrowing.
3. **Finance cost.** Finance cost is ₹125 cr against gross borrowings of ₹452 cr (S p.343–344). *Partly answered:* management cites bill-discounting charges and interest rate moves (Q3C p.13). Still no split, and it is unclear whether supplier finance costs are in this line. *Sharper in FY27:* finance cost ₹52.40 cr in Q2 on gross debt that averaged roughly ₹700 cr (₹452 cr → ≈₹918 cr) is ~30% annualised (our calc. from Q2R p.9–10). Interest on debt alone cannot explain it.
4. **"Other current financial assets" (₹1,009.61 cr)** (S p.343). *Largely answered:* the Q4 FY26 presentation counts ₹11 Bn of fixed deposits (maturity under 365 days) as cash, while the accounts hold them in other financial assets (Q4P p.20). The ₹15.3 Bn net cash depends on this.
5. **Who are the tube-mill vendors** behind the ₹603 cr single-vendor capex key audit matter, and are any related to the promoters? (S p.334)
6. **SG Green Logistics.** Why did logistics purchases from this promoter-linked company rise to ₹67 cr from ₹21 cr, and how is pricing benchmarked? (S p.399) → [[sg-green-logistics]]
7. **S Gupta Sports and Entertainment.** What was the ₹10 cr purchase of services for? (S p.399)
8. **Brand split.** *Volumes answered:* SG Premium volumes are now reported by quarter, e.g. 230,862 ton in FY26 and 58,686 in Q1 FY27 (Q1P p.12). Margin is still only management's claim, which has fallen from ₹1,500–2,000 to ₹0–1,000/ton (C23). → [[sg-premium]]
9. **Capex budget.** *Partly answered:* ₹13 Bn greenfield/brownfield plus ₹2 Bn debottlenecking by FY28 (Q3P p.12). Still needed: yearly phasing, and whether ₹5,000 per ton of capacity is realistic.
10. **Super-speciality JVs.** Who are the partners, what are the terms and the timing? Management promised clarity "in the next 2 to 3 quarters" from January 2026 (Q3C p.11; S p.20, p.105).
11. **Raipur tax benefit.** The scheme started in 2018 (Q3C p.14). When does the tax holiday end? (S p.152) → [[apl-apollo-building-products]]
12. **Auditor change.** Why did Deloitte step down after FY25 — routine rotation or something else? (S p.337)
13. **Blue Ocean Projects sale.** Who is the buyer, at what valuation, and is it a related party? (S p.153) *Partly answered:* sold for ₹160 cr (carrying ₹150.30 cr; gain ₹9.70 cr), completed in Q2 FY27 (Q2R p.12). **Buyer still not named.**
14. **Dealer volumes.** The annual report says top dealers each take 40,000–50,000 ton a month (S p.101). That looks inconsistent with top-10 dealers taking 44% of ~91% of ~3.5 Mn ton a year (~11,000–12,000 ton/month on average; our calculation from S p.210).
15. **Why was Q4 FY26 guidance missed so widely?** *Answered by management:* on track to 28 February 2026, then hit by the Middle East war (steel shortage, Dubai at 40%, destocking, gas shortage, labour) (Q4C p.3–4, p.10). Unverified: no monthly data before and after 28 February was given. Q4 volume was 925 thousand ton, against guidance of 10–10.5 lakh. That would require ~1.2–1.3 lakh ton of lost sales in March alone (our calculation).
16. **Guidance progress.** All `raw/` sources are ingested as of 2026-10-28 (to Q2 FY27, plus the ICRA rating and three broker notes). Next sources to find: the company's filed Q2 FY27 transcript, a dated share-price series, FY27 Q3 results (January 2027), and the FY26 shareholding pattern filings.
17. **Capacity timing.** Is 8 Mn ton by FY28 still the plan? The CMD's "2 and 2.5 years" from May 2026 and ₹500–600 cr a year of capex point to FY29, and the Q4 deck dropped plant tonnages and capex (Q4C p.4, p.6; Q4P p.12). → [[capital-allocation]]
18. **APL Apollo Mart's ₹111.72 cr net worth.** The company is described as non-operational since its 2021 incorporation, with ₹9.02 cr of FY26 revenue (Q4R p.6). What assets make up the net worth, and where do they go in liquidation? → [[apl-apollo-mart]] *Largely answered:* liquidation completed in Q2 FY27; APL Apollo received ₹109.36 cr (Q2R p.18), close to the ₹111.72 cr net worth. The assets were apparently mostly cash or liquid. Why a "non-operational" company held ~₹110 cr is still unexplained.
19. **"Better payment terms from the creditors" in Q4.** How much of the ~₹1,000 cr Q4 net cash increase came from longer payables or supplier finance, compared with profit (~₹350 cr) and inventory (~₹250 cr)? (Q4C p.7–8) → [[supplier-finance]]
20. **Revenue regrouping.** ₹6.19 Bn of traded-product sales was regrouped into Q1–Q3 FY26 (Q4P p.19). Were these sales previously netted, or reported elsewhere? Why is trading revenue (₹5.3 Bn in Q4) rising?
21. **How much of the Q4 margin is a one-off?** Rust-proof and Coated EBITDA/ton jumped to ₹7,261 and ₹8,047 in Q4 on shortage pricing (Q4P p.10; Q4C p.5). *Cannot now be checked:* the Q1 FY27 deck dropped product-line EBITDA/ton (Q1P p.12). Group EBITDA/ton held at ₹5,522, helped by a ~₹1,000/ton gross-profit gain from price increases ahead of steel costs (Q1C p.4).
22. **Why was product-line margin disclosure stopped?** The CMD says competitors used it against the company (Q1C p.17). It stopped in the same quarter that management's own numbers for the January 2025 price increase and the SG Premium margin were restated lower (C22, C23). Will any margin split be given again, for example in the annual report?
23. **Q1 inventory build.** Inventory rose from ₹14,453 Mn to ₹19,015 Mn (+₹4.6 Bn) while volume fell 19% QoQ (Q1P p.20). How much is the ~70,000 ton in transit to Dubai (Q1C p.6), how much is unsold domestic stock, and was it bought before the April HRC price rise? This turned operating cash flow negative (−₹0.3 Bn).
24. **Group Shared Services Company.** Who owns the other ~80%? What fees will APL Apollo pay, on what basis, and will they be approved as related-party transactions? (Q1R p.1; Q1C p.15) → [[group-shared-services-company]] *Partly answered:* the company is SG Enterprise Solutions Pvt Ltd (incorporated 10 Sep 2026). APL Apollo is buying 31% from S Gupta Holding and SG Green Buildings to reach 51%, making it a subsidiary by 31 Dec 2026 (Q2R p.1, p.3–4). Fee basis still unknown; who holds the remaining 49% is not stated.
25. **Apollo Metalex A-25 land sale.** What is the book value and expected sale value of the land and building? Who is the buyer? (Q1R p.2) → [[apollo-metalex]] *Partly answered:* sale agreement signed with an unnamed "third party"; ₹15.59 cr book value held for sale (Q2R p.12). Sale price not disclosed.
26. **Volume path to FY27 guidance.** The CMD's path is Q2 10, Q3 10.5 and Q4 12 lakh ton (Q1C p.18). Q4 at 12 lakh would be +30% YoY on 925 thousand ton, and above Q3's target by 14% (our calculation). It also needs Gorakhpur and Siliguri to ramp up within months of starting. Check against Q2 actuals.
27. **The ₹500 cr liability.** Debt fell only ~₹23 cr in Q1, and interest cost rose (Q1P p.19–20). Which liability is meant? Debt is only ~₹429 cr, so it is probably supplier finance or acceptances (see Q2). Is it still due to be retired by Q2? → [[supplier-finance]] *Worse in Q2:* gross debt rose to ≈₹918 cr, with net current borrowings of +₹538 cr in H1 (Q2R p.10–11), while cash and FDs also rose. Why borrow short-term while holding ~₹2,500 cr of cash and FDs? Was supplier finance or acceptance credit moved into bank borrowings? *Confirmed by a second source:* borrowings ₹452.5 → ₹918.1 cr (Q2N p.1). See also the ICRA limit shift in Q2.
28. **UAE revenue per ton.** The UAE subsidiary reported Q2 revenue of ₹425.01 cr (Q2R p.7) on 26,227 ton (Q2P p.13), about ₹1.62 lakh/ton, against ~₹81,000 in FY26 (our calc.). Is it selling the ~70,000 ton in transit as trading, without counting it as volume? → [[apl-apollo-tubes-company-llc]]
29. **Where did the margin come from in Q2?** The CSO says gross profit per ton fell ~₹500 QoQ, so the ₹477/ton EBITDA rise was costs and operating leverage (Q2C 21:45). The press release credits value-added mix (Q2P p.36). How much is operating leverage that reverses if volume falls?
30. **Brand + Roofing growth claim.** The CSO says the two grew ~20% YoY in H1 (Q2C 01:16). Reported volumes give +5.3% (brand +3.8%, Roofing +15.2%; our calc. from Q2P p.13). See C27.
31. **Blue Ocean buyer and proceeds.** Who bought it? Was ₹160 cr fully received? The Q1 note says the consideration was received after 30 June (Q1R p.8).
32. **SG Green Buildings.** Not previously in the wiki. What does it do, and does it have other dealings with APL Apollo? (Q2R p.3)
33. **Light-structure reclassification.** Light structures are now 25% of the APL Apollo brand mix (Q2C 22:29). Were they moved from another category in the restated volume table? Is the brand's growth partly a reclassification?
34. **Which net-cash figure is right?** Company: ₹16.3 Bn at Sep 2026, up from ₹15.3 Bn, counting ~₹14 Bn of FDs as cash (Q2P p.18, p.20). Systematix: ₹2.3 Bn, down from ₹4.3 Bn, excluding those FDs (SY p.1). How liquid are the FDs? Are any lien-marked against the borrowings or supplier-finance lines? → [[financials]]
35. **Capacity: 6.8 or 8 MTPA by FY28?** ICRA's February 2026 rationale has ~6.8 MTPA by FY2028 (ICRA p.1), built from the plants the company gave it. The company says 8 Mn ton. Which plants make up the 1.2 MTPA difference? → [[capital-allocation]]
36. **Origin of the unattributed results note.** `raw/Broker reports/2026-10-q2-fy27-results-note.pdf` names no firm or author. Who wrote it? Its call summary is used as a cross-check on the machine transcript.
37. **Ownership of SG Finserve, SG Mart and Apollo Pipes.** These listed companies share directors with APL Apollo and will use the shared-services company, but the ingested sources do not state that they are promoter-owned (S p.130, p.166; Q1C p.15). Their shareholding filings would settle it. → [[promoter-group-companies]]
