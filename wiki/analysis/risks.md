# Risks — Apl Apollo Tubes Ltd

## What the company reports

The FY26 annual report lists nine principal risks under a COSO ERM framework, with a Risk Management Committee and three lines of defence ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.122–125):

| # | Risk (company wording, shortened) | Company's stated trend | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Competitive intensity, pricing premium and market share (organised capacity, primary-steel downstream entry, imports) | Increasing | p.124 |
| 2 | Raw material, steel cycle, secondary steel competition and trade policy | Increasing/Stable* | p.124 |
| 3 | Demand, channel liquidity and market concentration | Stable* | p.124 |
| 4 | Growth execution and capital allocation (delays, overruns, slow ramp-up) | Stable* | p.124 |
| 5 | Geopolitical, supply chain and international business (UAE, GCC, exports) — emerging | Increasing* | p.125 |
| 6 | Operational resilience, product quality and safety | Stable* | p.125 |
| 7 | Climate transition, carbon (CBAM/CCTS) and physical climate — emerging | Increasing* | p.125 |
| 8 | Digital, cyber and AI — emerging | Increasing* | p.125 |
| 9 | Working capital and liquidity | — | p.125 |

*Trend arrows were extracted from a graphic; the assignment to individual risks is **low confidence**.

Other risks mentioned by management: Middle East conflict and India–Pakistan border tensions hit FY26 volumes; gas/energy shortages and steel supply disruptions in Q4 FY26; channel destocking for ~18 months (p.49, p.54); "Steel prices remain volatile. Geopolitical conditions are uncertain" (p.22).

## Our read

Ranked by what this source suggests matters most for the equity:

1. **Spread/EBITDA-per-ton reversion.** FY26 EBITDA/ton (₹5,161) is 36% above FY25. History shows it can fall below ₹4,000 (p.25). Guidance of +20–25% EBITDA growth assumes it holds. See [[ebitda-per-ton]].
2. **Balance sheet quality is weaker than the "net cash" headline.** ₹1,382 cr of [[supplier-finance]] payables (p.386). If banks pull back these lines, working capital would need roughly that much cash. *Update Oct 2026:* gross borrowings doubled to ≈₹918 cr in H1 FY27. On a strict basis excluding FDs, Systematix puts net cash at only ₹2.3 Bn ([[src-2026-10-systematix-q2-fy27-note]], p.1). [[icra]]'s downgrade trigger is debt/OPBITDA above 0.75× sustained; it is ~0.47× now (our calc.; [[src-2026-02-icra-rating]], p.3).
3. **Execution on 3 Mn ton of new capacity by FY28** (timing now more likely FY29; see risk 12) with four greenfield sites and unnamed JV partners (p.5, p.20). Average utilisation is already only ~70% (p.148), so new capacity needs demand to grow into.
4. **Channel concentration.** Dealers are ~91% of sales, and the top 10 take 44% of dealer sales (p.210).
5. **Governance.** Single-vendor capex (₹603 cr KAM), rising promoter-linked RPTs, family pay, auditor change (p.334, p.399–400). See [[governance]].
6. **HRC price falls and unhedged inventory** (p.180), plus a possible end of the 12% safeguard duty (p.50).
7. **UAE / Middle East exposure** through the Dubai plant (~₹1,728 cr revenue) (p.337). This risk materialised in March–May 2026: Dubai fell to ~40% utilisation ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]], p.4).
8. **Disclosure reliability.** The report contradicts itself on several items (see [[open-questions]]). This says something about the quality of the narrative sections.
9. **Guidance credibility.** In January 2026 management raised guidance (Q4 FY26 +20%, FY27 at least 4.2 Mn ton and at least ₹5,500/ton) and said it would not cut again. Q4 and FY26 were missed and FY27 guidance was restated lower in May ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-concall]], p.5–11; [[guidance-tracker]]). Treat management's numbers as upper bounds until a track record builds.
10. **Low-price volume push via [[sg-premium]].** Management is willing to sell SG Premium "whatever the price", possibly below HRC cost ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-concall]], p.18). This could dilute EBITDA/ton and invite price competition in the base segment.
11. **Shortage-driven margins may not last.** The Q4 FY26 record EBITDA/ton (₹5,525) was helped by war-related steel and gas shortages that lifted galvanised and coated spreads ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]], p.5). If supply normalises and management "adjusts the margin a little" to recover volume (p.5), EBITDA/ton could fall back towards ₹5,000.
12. **Capacity timing slip.** The CMD's "2 and 2.5 years" for the remaining capex points to FY29, not FY28 ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]], p.6). In August the CSO repeated "two and a half years" ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.4). See [[capital-allocation]].
13. **Back-ended FY27 guidance.** After Q1 volume of 745 thousand ton (−6% YoY), the 15% target needs +21% YoY over Q2–Q4. Management's path is 10, 10.5 and 12 lakh ton, with Q4 relying on new plants that start in September and Q4 ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.18; our calculation). A third consecutive miss would further damage credibility (see risk 9). *Q2 update:* Q2 came close (963 thousand ton), but in October the company-wide volume guidance was in effect withdrawn. Only the APL Apollo brand and Roofing are now committed ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 06:31).
14. **Cash conversion reversal.** Operating cash flow was −₹30 cr in Q1 FY27 on a ₹456 cr inventory build, and net cash fell to ₹14.1 Bn ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-presentation]], p.17–20). Interest cost rose 23% QoQ against a promise of "almost zero". *Q2 update:* operating cash flow recovered (statutory H1 ₹594 cr), but gross debt doubled to ≈₹918 cr and Q2 interest was ₹52 cr ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.9–11). The presentation's operating cash flow runs ~13% above the statutory figure (C28).
15. **Shrinking disclosure and new related-party vehicles.** Product-line margins were withdrawn ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.17), and a promoter-group shared-services company was created ([[group-shared-services-company]]). Both make the business harder to monitor from outside. *Q2 update:* the SSC is to become a 51% subsidiary, with the extra shares bought from promoter entities ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.3), and the heavy/super-heavy split is also no longer given ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 42:37).
16. **Margin depends on operating leverage.** Q2 FY27 EBITDA/ton of ₹5,999 came with gross profit per ton ~₹500 lower QoQ ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 21:45). The gain came from fixed-cost absorption, which reverses if volume falls. The new ₹6,000 target therefore assumes volume holds.
17. **Source and presentation reliability.** In Q2 FY27 the presentation overstated revenue growth against the filing (+34% vs +26.6%) and operating cash flow (₹683 cr vs ₹594 cr), and printed an obvious EBITDA/ton typo. The only Q2 call transcript is an unofficial machine transcript (C28, C29). Rely on filed statements. Broker notes also contain errors: Axis gives roofing full-capacity volume as ~75 kt a quarter instead of ~175 kt ([[src-2026-10-axis-q2-fy27-note]], p.2).
