# Capital allocation

## What the company reports

- **Framework:** management describes a "four-bucket" allocation across taxes, growth capex, shareholder returns and reserves/balance sheet strengthening ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.69, p.75).
- **Capex plan:** capacity from 5 to 8 Mn ton by FY28, "funded entirely by internal cash flows, without debt or equity dilution" (p.5, p.18). Components:
  - ~1 Mn ton from debottlenecking existing plants (p.7).
  - Greenfield plants at Gorakhpur (UP), Siliguri (West Bengal), New Malur (Karnataka), and an export-dedicated plant in the "Western Coastal area" (p.9, p.20, p.68).
  - Dubai facility expansion to 0.5 Mn ton (p.20).
  - Beyond FY28: 10 Mn ton by 2030, the extra 2 Mn ton "dedicated entirely to super-speciality segments", via JVs with manufacturers in Japan, Korea, Europe and the US (p.20, p.105).
- **Plant-by-plant plan and cost (Jan 2026):**

- **Balance sheet goal:** move from "debt-free to liability-free". "The residual ₹500 crore current liability will be retired in FY27" (p.20–21). Management says it pays suppliers in advance to capture cash discounts (p.21).
- **Shareholder returns:**
  - Final dividend ₹8.50/share for FY26 (₹236.01 cr) vs ₹5.75 for FY25 (₹159.64 cr) (p.153, p.410).
  - "The dividend payout floor has been raised to 20%." Once the balance sheet "enters its permanent net-cash phase in FY27", allocation will shift "toward shareholders through enhanced dividends, share buybacks, or a combination of both" (p.21).
  - The Dividend Distribution Policy was amended on 7 May 2025 so the payout ratio is based on consolidated profits (p.152).
- **Buyback signal (May 2026):** "I have a liability of INR500 crores. Once I eliminate this liability in Q1 and Q2, I'm unsure what to do with the cash. Either increase the dividend or do a buyback." Asked whether to expect a good dividend this year: "I can say yes." — Sanjay Gupta ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]], p.7).
- **Asset recycling:** in-principle approval (2 May 2026) to divest [[blue-ocean-projects]], a real-estate holding subsidiary, "to unlock capital and redeploy into the core manufacturing business" (p.153). The Shankara Building Products stake was sold in FY26 (p.369).
- **Return hurdle:** management says it has a 35% internal ROCE benchmark (p.18), and a director is quoted: "whatever we do, we will ensure that it is ROCE-accretive" (p.61).

## Our read

- The "net cash ₹15.3 Bn" and "liability-free" claims need to be read alongside ₹1,382 cr of payables under [[supplier-finance]] (p.386). If that is treated as quasi-debt, the net-cash position largely disappears. The "₹500 crore current liability" to be retired may be part of this, but the report does not say.
- **Possible timing slip:** at ₹500–600 cr a year, the ₹1,400–1,500 cr remaining capex takes about 2.5–3 years. The CMD's "2 and 2.5 years" from May 2026 ends between May and November 2028, i.e. in FY29. That sits uneasily with "8 Mn ton by FY28". The Q4 deck also removed the plant-level detail. Watch the next updates. See [[guidance-tracker]].
- **August 2026:** the CSO's "two and a half years" from August 2026 ends around February 2029, which confirms the FY29 reading. The deck still says FY28 (see C21 and C25 in [[open-questions]]). The plan has also been reshaped since January: Malur from 0.6 to ~1 Mn ton, Bhuj dropped for Pune, and the Raipur 0.6 Mn ton brownfield no longer mentioned. The total still adds to 2 Mn ton.
- **Shareholder returns in Q1 FY27:** no buyback or dividend increase, and the ₹500 cr liability was not mentioned on the call. Net cash fell ₹1.26 Bn (₹126 cr) ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-presentation]], p.18). The May promise to retire the liability "in Q1 and Q2" now depends on Q2 ([[guidance-tracker]]).
- **October 2026:** H2 capex of "hardly Rs 200 crore" after ~₹400 cr in H1 sits oddly with plants supposed to add 2 Mn ton within two years. Either much of the ₹1,400–1,500 cr is already spent or prepaid as capital advances, or the build-out is slower (our read). Asset sales returned ~₹270 cr of cash. Meanwhile gross debt doubled, so the "liability-free" goal moved further away in H1.
- ₹1,500 cr for 3 Mn ton works out to ~₹5,000 per ton of annual capacity (our calculation). That is very cheap. Watch whether actual spend in FY27–28 stays near this figure, given the single-vendor capex key audit matter ([[governance]]).
- The 20% payout floor (p.21) is low for a company that says it is generating surplus cash. The buyback option is a promise to track in [[guidance-tracker]].
