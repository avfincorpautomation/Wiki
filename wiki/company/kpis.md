## Our read

- FY25 was the trough for EBITDA/ton (₹3,797; p.25). FY26 recovered above FY23–24 levels but stayed below FY22 (₹5,386).
- Value-added share fell every quarter (61% → 55%) while EBITDA/ton rose. The product table above shows why: the largest line, General, went from ₹1,705/ton in FY25 to ₹3,149 in Q3 FY26, and every value-added line also rose by ₹1,000–2,000/ton. The gain was broad pricing, not mix, which fits management's claim of a ₹3,000–4,000/ton brand premium that "is now the new normal" ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-concall]], p.3).
- Volume growth slowed from Q3's +11% to Q4's +9% just after management guided +20% for Q4 (see [[guidance-tracker]]). April 2026 volume was 2.5 lakh ton, roughly flat to down on the Q1 FY26 monthly average of ~2.6 lakh ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]], p.11).
- The Q4 FY26 record of ₹5,525/ton came partly from war-related shortages, which management says lifted galvanised and coated margins ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]], p.5). Rust-proof (₹7,261) and Coated (₹8,047) were well above their FY26 averages. Not all of the Q4 margin should be treated as recurring.
- **Q1 FY27:** EBITDA/ton held at ₹5,522 on 19% lower volume. Management credits a ~₹1,000/ton gross-profit gain from raising prices ahead of steel costs ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.4). That is a pricing choice, made at the cost of volume. By category, the drops were concentrated where APL Apollo competes on price ([[sg-premium]] −35% QoQ) and in the UAE (−48% QoQ). Roofing grew. Because product-line margins are no longer published, we can no longer check whether the Q4 shortage margins in Rust-proof and Coated held.

> [!warning] Contradiction
> Market share and value-added share. FY26 market share was reported as 65%, up from 55% ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.24), but in August the CMD described 60–65% as a target "from 50% to 55%" ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.8). Value-added share was 55–61% by FY26 quarter (p.51–54), against "like 65%" now ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.4). The definitions may differ (for example, Roofing counted as value-added). See C24 and C26 in [[open-questions]].
