- "We are debt-free today, but we want to be liability-free." — Deepak Goyal (p.30).
- "Being a dominant brand, our pricing power is strong." — Rahul Gupta (p.40).
- On guidance (Q3 FY26 call, 22 Jan 2026): after earlier cuts "we were hurt badly … we are not ready to take back the guidelines at any cost" — Sanjay Gupta ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-concall]], p.9). The guidance given on that call was missed for Q4 FY26 and restated lower in May 2026 (see [[guidance-tracker]]).
- On the Q4 FY26 call (4 May 2026) the CSO described the January guidance as "near about 1 million ton of sales volume in quarter four with INR5,300-INR5,500 per ton EBITDA", and said "if war had not started, we would have met our guidance" ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]], p.10). The January transcript has ₹5,500 as a "minimum". Management's restatements of its own past guidance should be checked against the original transcript.
- Anubhav Gupta (CSO) does most of the numerical guidance on calls. The CMD's answers are given largely in Hindi and are hard to follow in the translated transcript ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-concall]]).

## Our read

- Executive control sits with the promoter family. The CMD drew no salary in FY26 (FY25: ₹4.39 cr), while two family members classed as non-executive directors drew ~₹6.8–6.9 cr each (p.400). See [[governance]] for why this matters.
- The professional bench (CFO, COO, CSO, Dubai CEO) is visible but newer to the board; C.K. Singh only joined the board in July 2025.
- The pattern from January to August 2026 is confident targets, a miss, and the guidance kept. The Q1 FY27 call kept FY27 guidance after a 15% miss on management's own Q1 volume target, and loaded the year onto Q4 ("Our main game play will be Q4") ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.18). See [[guidance-tracker]].
- From January to October 2026 the headline target moved from volume (20%, at least 4.2 Mn ton) to absolute EBITDA (20%), and now to EBITDA/ton (₹6,000+). Each change came after the earlier metric fell short.
