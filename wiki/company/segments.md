# Segments

## What the company reports

- **One reportable segment:** "The Group is in business of Manufacturing of ERW steel tube and pipes and hence only one reportable operating segment as per Ind-AS 108" ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.362).
- **Geography (revenue from contracts with customers):** India ₹20,368.26 cr (FY25: ₹18,600.38 cr); outside India ₹2,015.36 cr (FY25: ₹1,395.91 cr), about 9% of contract revenue in FY26 (p.388).
- **Product mix (management):** value-added products ~58% of FY26 sales (p.148). Quarterly value-added share: Q1 61%, Q2 57%, Q3 57%, Q4 55% (p.51–54). See [[value-added-products]].
- **End-use mix FY26:** housing 67%, commercial 19%, infrastructure 10%, industrial & agriculture 4% (p.152). The 9M FY26 split by revenue was housing 64%, commercial 19%, infrastructure 13%, others 4% ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-presentation]], p.22). The Q4 FY26 presentation gives the same 64/19/13/4 split for full-year FY26 revenue ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-presentation]], p.22).

> [!warning] Contradiction
> FY26 end-use mix: the Board's Report gives housing 67% and infrastructure 10% ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.152); the Q4 presentation gives housing 64% and infrastructure 13% "as per FY26 revenue" (p.22). The annual report's basis is not stated. See [[open-questions]].
- **Product lines:** the presentation splits volume and EBITDA/ton into six lines (Heavy, Light, General, Rust-proof, Coated, Galv). See the table in [[kpis]].

  - [[apl-apollo-building-products]] and [[apollo-metalex]]: jointly audited; combined revenue ₹8,160.26 cr, PAT ₹573.01 cr, total assets ₹4,105.49 cr (p.337).

  - [[blue-ocean-projects]] and [[apl-apollo-mart]]: small (p.350, p.414).
  - Standalone parent: revenue ₹15,109.94 cr, PAT ₹547.12 cr (p.151).
- From Q1 FY27 the presentation reports volume in four categories: APL Apollo brand, SG Premium brand, UAE operations and Roofing products, restated back to FY24 ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-presentation]], p.12). Q1 FY27: 569 / 59 / 26 / 92 thousand ton of 745. Full table in [[kpis]]. There is still one reportable segment under Ind AS 108 ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-results]], p.8).
- UAE subsidiary, Q1 FY27 (reviewed by another auditor): revenue ₹289.01 cr, PAT ₹1.62 cr ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-results]], p.5; Q1 PAT as implied by [[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.7). Q2 FY27: revenue ₹425.01 cr, PAT ₹26.86 cr; H1 revenue ₹714.02 cr, PAT ₹28.48 cr ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.7).

## Our read

- The parent earned ~45% of group PAT on ~65% of group revenue, while the Raipur/Metalex subsidiaries earned ~48% of group PAT (our calculation, p.151, p.337). That fits management's claim that Raipur value-added products lift margins (p.52) and its mention of Raipur tax benefits (p.152). Intercompany sales mean these shares are approximate.
- Volume and EBITDA/ton by product line are now available from the Q3 FY26 presentation. There is still no split by brand (SG Premium sits inside "General"). Gap logged in [[open-questions]].
- The new categories are a brand/geography split, not a product split. Combined with the end of product-line EBITDA/ton, it is now harder to see where margin comes from.
