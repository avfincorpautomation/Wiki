## Our read

- The multiples above are facts at the dates stated. Whether they are high or low is the analyst's call in [[thesis]].
- **Sensitivities worth noting.** These are arithmetic, not a view. Both broker targets depend on FY28 EBITDA/ton of about ₹6,000 and volume of 4.4–4.6 Mt. At ICRA's ₹5,000/ton on 4.4 Mt, FY28 EBITDA would be ~₹2,200 cr instead of ₹2,600–2,800 cr (our calculation).
- **The net-cash figure feeds the EV.** The company's ₹16.3 Bn counts FDs, while Systematix's ₹2.3 Bn excludes them (see [[financials]]). The EV in the results note (₹61,207 cr, i.e. ₹232 cr below the market cap) implies they also used the strict net-cash figure (our inference).
