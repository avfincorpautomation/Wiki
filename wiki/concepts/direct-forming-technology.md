# Direct Forming Technology (DFT)

**What it is:** a tube-forming method that shapes square and rectangular hollow sections directly from steel strip, rather than first forming a round tube and then reshaping it. Size changes need fewer roll changes, so short production runs are cheaper.

## What the company reports

- Management says it "pioneered Direct Forming Technology in India" more than a decade ago, cutting raw material wastage ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.20, p.38).
- DFT mills are installed "at almost all the plants" and produce 80×80 mm to 200×200 mm sections up to 10 mm thick. Management says the benefits are less manpower, higher productivity and lower inventory (p.164).
- The company claims to be the first in India, and the second in the world, to make 1,000 mm × 1,000 mm structural tubes (p.20, p.35).

## Our read

DFT supports the many-SKU, low-inventory model. Whether competitors now use it widely is not covered by this source. A competitor source would answer this.
