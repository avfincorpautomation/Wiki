# EBITDA per ton

**What it is:** EBITDA divided by tons sold. It is the main unit-profit metric for Indian steel tube makers, because revenue per ton moves with steel prices while the conversion spread is what the business controls.

## What the company reports

- APL Apollo's definition: EBITDA excluding other income ÷ sales volume. The KPI chart checks out: e.g. FY26 ₹18,019 Mn ÷ 3,491 thousand ton = ₹5,161 ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.24–25).
- History: FY22 ₹5,386; FY23 ₹4,481; FY24 ₹4,553; FY25 ₹3,797; FY26 ₹5,161 (p.25). Quarterly FY26: ₹4,683 / ₹5,228 / ₹5,146 / ₹5,525 (p.51–54).
- Medium-term target: ₹5,300–5,700 (p.20).
- Q1 FY27: ₹5,522/ton (Q4 FY26 ₹5,525; Q1 FY26 ₹4,683) ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-presentation]], p.8).
- Management targets, August 2026: ₹5,000–5,500 "throughout the year"; "no point in going below 5,000"; +₹100–200/ton a year; ₹6,000/ton at 8 Mn ton in "two, three years" ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.4, p.11, p.18).
- Q2 FY27: **₹5,999** (Q1 ₹5,522; Q2 FY26 ₹5,228); H1 ₹5,791 ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-presentation]], p.7–9).
- October management view: ₹6,000 "sustainable"; "Rs 6,000-plus in Q3"; guidance to be revised after two quarters at ₹6,000+ ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 01:16, 26:07, 38:51). The CSO says gross profit per ton fell ~₹500 QoQ (light-structure mix), so the gain came from operating leverage and costs (21:45).
- **External expectations:** [[icra]] ₹4,500–5,000 medium term ([[src-2026-02-icra-rating]], p.1). Axis FY27E ₹5,739 ([[src-2026-10-axis-q2-fy27-note]], p.4). Systematix FY27E–29E ₹5,958 / ₹5,968 / ₹6,002 ([[src-2026-10-systematix-q2-fy27-note]], p.2).

## Our read

- Comparisons across companies need the same definition (with or without other income; per ton sold or per ton produced).
- At APL Apollo, mix (value-added share), brand premium, and inventory gains or losses on HRC price moves all feed this number. A quarter with rising HRC prices can flatter it.
- Q1 held EBITDA/ton by giving up volume. Management has now cut prices on "15% to 20% of our total basket" to win volume back ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.16). Q2 will show whether ₹5,000+ survives.
- The Q2 rise came from spreading fixed costs over 29% more volume, not from a better spread. If volume falls back, so does EBITDA/ton. See C30 in [[open-questions]].
- The gap between ICRA (₹4,500–5,000) and management and brokers (₹6,000) is the core valuation sensitivity. See [[valuation]] and C35 in [[open-questions]].
