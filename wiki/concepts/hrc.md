# HRC (hot-rolled coil)

**What it is:** flat steel rolled at high temperature and coiled. It is the main raw material for ERW steel tubes. HRC prices set the cost base for APL Apollo and its peers.

## What the company reports

- FY26 standalone HRC purchases: ₹9,004.26 cr for 18,02,264 ton, about ₹49,960/ton (our calculation); no commodity derivatives used ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.180).
- Management says HRC prices trended down in FY26 as domestic steel capacity grew, and this led channel partners to destock for "nearly a year and a half" (p.49).
- India imposed a 12% safeguard duty on steel imports, which management says limited the downside on domestic prices and helped end destocking (p.50, p.152).
- Management says the company refuses to use sponge-iron-based secondary steel (p.21). See [[secondary-steel-patra]].

## Our read

Falling HRC prices hurt through inventory losses and channel destocking, even with [[pass-through-pricing]]. The status of the safeguard duty is a policy variable to track; no expiry date was found in this source.
