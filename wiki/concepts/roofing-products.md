# Roofing products

**What it is:** coated and galvanised steel roofing sheets and related building products, sold under the APL Apollo brand. Thickness runs from about 0.4 mm to 3.5 mm ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 12:44). Since August 2026 Roofing is one of the four volume categories the company reports, alongside the APL Apollo brand, [[sg-premium]] and UAE operations ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-presentation]], p.12).

## What the company reports

- **Where it is made:** Raipur ([[apl-apollo-building-products]]) is described as the "national hub for value-added products and roofing solutions", with "ongoing innovation in capsule roofing" ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.68, p.70). The annual report names roofing and light structures among the underserved East India markets targeted by the new capacity (p.9).
- **Volume ('000 ton)** ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-presentation]], p.13; [[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-presentation]], p.12):

  | FY24 | FY25 | FY26 | Q1 FY26 | Q2 FY26 | Q3 FY26 | Q4 FY26 | Q1 FY27 | Q2 FY27 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 273 | 358 | 390 | 92 | 105 | 106 | 87 | 92 | 135 |

  Roofing was ~11% of FY26 volume and 14% of Q2 FY27 volume. H1 FY27 was +15.2% YoY (our calc.).
- **Capacity:** 50,000–60,000 ton a month, ~7 lakh ton a year ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 11:14). That is ~175 kt a quarter at full use ([[src-2026-10-systematix-q2-fy27-note]], p.3). New lines were commissioned in July 2026 ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results-note]], p.4). In August a new roofing line was expected to add 20,000 ton ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.6).
- **End use:** 50% housing, 25% commercial and industrial, 25% other, including solar and insulated panels (Q2 call 12:25; results note p.4).
- **Margin (management claim):** "For Apollo and roofing products, we are targeting an EBITDA of INR6,000 per ton to INR7,000 per ton … right now, we are operating in the range of INR6,000 to INR7,000" ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.17). No reported figure exists.
- **Q1 FY27:** the energy crisis cost 25,000–30,000 ton across rust-proof and roofing products ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.3).
- **Q2 FY27:** management credits roofing as one of the drivers of the ₹5,999/ton EBITDA ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 37:24). Together, the APL Apollo brand and Roofing are "almost 92–93%" of volume (43:55).

## Our read

- Roofing is now the growth engine management points to. Q2 FY27 volume was +29% YoY and +48% QoQ (our calc.), the fastest of the four categories.
- The ₹6,000–7,000/ton margin is a management claim since product-line disclosure ended (see [[kpis]]). It cannot be checked against reported figures.
- At ~135 kt a quarter against ~175 kt of capacity, roofing is at roughly 77% use (our calc.). The "1,75,000 tonnes" mentioned on the call is therefore close to full capacity, not just a target.
