# Value-added products (VAP)

**What it is:** products that earn a higher spread than plain black or general structural tubes: galvanised, pre-galvanised, coated, heavy and super-heavy sections, roofing, "Steel for Green" building products, and similar.

## What the company reports

- VAP share of sales: ~58% in FY26 ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.32, p.148). Quarterly: 61%, 57%, 57%, 55% (p.51–54).
- The MD&A cites industry estimates that VAP earns 1.2–1.3x higher realisations and 9–12% EBITDA margins (p.145).
- Raipur ([[apl-apollo-building-products]]) is the main VAP hub (p.68).
- The planned "super-speciality" segment (EVs, aerospace, petrochemicals, oil & gas), to come via JVs, is pitched as the next tier above today's VAP (p.20, p.105).

- **Definition used in the Q3 FY26 presentation:** "Value added products with EBITDA more than Rs 5,000/Ton" ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-presentation]], p.10). Q3 FY26 EBITDA/ton by line: Heavy ₹8,767, Coated ₹7,011, Rust-proof ₹6,324, Light ₹6,023, Galv ₹5,398, against General (non-VAP) ₹3,149 (p.10). Full table in [[kpis]].
- August 2026: the value-added share "right now is like 65%", rising to 75–80% at 8 Mn ton. General products were ~35% of Q1 FY27 volume ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.4, p.16). The CMD expects 75–80% by Q4 FY28, driven by New Malur (~1 Mn ton, EBITDA "8,000 plus") (p.9–10).

> [!warning] Contradiction
> ~58% of FY26 sales ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.11–14), and 55–61% by FY26 quarter (p.51–54), against "like 65%" in August ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.4). See C24 in [[open-questions]].
- **Redefined (October 2026):** "Barring SG Premium, everything is value-added" — Anubhav Gupta ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 50:18). On that basis the Q2 FY27 value-added share is ~97% (our calc. from [[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-presentation]], p.13). The old definition (product lines with EBITDA above ₹5,000/ton) gave 55–61% in FY26.

## Our read

The VAP definition (EBITDA above ₹5,000/ton; [[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-presentation]], p.11) is based on margin, not on product type. A product could therefore move in or out of "value-added" as spreads change. The quarterly VAP share fell during FY26 while EBITDA/ton rose, so VAP mix was not the main driver this year (see [[kpis]]).
- The jump from ~58% to ~65% in one quarter is more likely a definition change, or a mix effect from the SG Premium volume collapse (SG Premium is in "General"), than real product progress. Volume definitions also changed in the same deck.
- The value-added share is now a definition, not a measurement. It can no longer be used as a trend indicator. Use the brand/Roofing/SG/UAE volume split in [[kpis]] instead.
