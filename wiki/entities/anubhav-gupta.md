# Anubhav Gupta — Chief Strategy Officer

## What the company reports

- **Role:** Chief Strategy Officer; part of senior management, not a board director ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.135–136, p.174).
- **Joined:** 26 October 2019. His remit covers strategy, evaluating new opportunities, corporate development, communications and marketing of the APL Apollo brand. He has "over 14 years of experience in fundamental research and analysis in the investment world, including leadership positions across investment advisory and investment banking firms" (p.174).
- Investor-relations contact on the earnings presentations ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-presentation]], p.35).
- Quoted in the annual report on the "perfect pyramid" growth model (p.16).
- The annual report does not show his remuneration in the related-party salary table, which lists KMP only (p.400). He is not designated KMP (p.398).

## On the calls

He gives the opening remarks and most of the numerical guidance on earnings calls:
- **January 2026:**
  - 20% volume growth in Q4 FY26 and FY27 (missed / superseded).
  - Interest cost to "almost zero levels" from Q1 FY27 (missed) ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-concall]], p.3, p.6, p.13).
- **May 2026:** "We are not reducing our guidance as of now … for the absolute EBITDA". 8 Mn ton by FY28 "remains totally on-track" ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]], p.4, p.12).
- **August 2026:**
  - "we chose to focus on profitability".
  - EBITDA/ton of ₹5,000–5,500 "throughout the year".
  - New capacity "over the next two and a half years" ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.4).
- **October 2026** (low-confidence transcript):
  - EBITDA/ton of ₹6,000 "is now sustainable".
  - "Barring SG Premium, everything is value-added".
  - Gross profit per ton ~₹500 lower QoQ.
  - Capacity "in the next two years" ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 01:16, 21:45, 50:18).
