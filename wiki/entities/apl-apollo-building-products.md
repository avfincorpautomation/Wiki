# APL Apollo Building Products Limited (ABPL)

## What the company reports

- Wholly owned subsidiary that runs the Raipur plant (village Rigni, Simga, Baloda Bazar, Chhattisgarh) ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.136).
- Converted from a private to a public limited company on 4 April 2025 (p.153).
- Raipur is described as the "all-India hub for value-added products and roofing" (p.68). Management also says the Raipur plant gets tax benefits for new facilities (p.152).
- Jointly audited by Walker Chandiok and VAPS & Company. Together with [[apollo-metalex]]: total assets ₹4,105.49 cr, revenue ₹8,160.26 cr, PAT ₹573.01 cr in FY26 (p.180, p.337).
- Its term loans are secured on Raipur/Baloda Bazar assets and guaranteed by the parent (p.379–381).
- Brajesh Nahar is listed as Director – ABPL (p.135).

## Our read

ABPL plus Metalex earned about half of group PAT (our calculation). Its tax-holiday position matters for the group tax rate. The expiry date is not in this source.
