# APL Apollo Mart Limited (AAML)

## What the company reports

- Wholly owned subsidiary ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.153, p.350).
- On 2 May 2026 the Board "took note of and considered the initiation of voluntary liquidation" of AAML under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (p.153).
- Audit fee paid to the statutory auditor: ₹0.025 cr (p.180).
- Per the Board outcome filing: AAML is a public limited company "incorporated in the year 2021 and is engaged in the business of trading and distribution of Steel and related products". It "has remained non-operational since incorporation and has undertaken only limited operations in the recent years", and is "an additional corporate layer without commensurate business value". FY26 revenue ₹9.02 cr (0.04% of consolidated turnover); **net worth ₹111.72 cr (2.11% of consolidated net worth)** ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-results]], p.6).
- **Liquidated.** The liquidation process was completed in Q2 FY27, and APL Apollo received **₹109.36 cr**. A dissolution application is pending before the NCLT, New Delhi ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.18). The consolidated note dates completion to "the quarter ended September 30, 2025", which is apparently a typo for 2026 (p.12).

## Our read

This is a clean-up of a non-core entity. But a "non-operational" company with ₹111.72 cr of net worth ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-results]], p.6) needs explaining: what assets does it hold, and where do they go on liquidation? See [[open-questions]].
- Proceeds of ₹109.36 cr against ₹111.72 cr of net worth suggest the entity held mostly cash or receivables. Why a company described as non-operational held that much is still unexplained.
