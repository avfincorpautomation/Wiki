# APL Apollo Tubes Company LLC (Dubai / Umm Al Quwain, UAE)

## What the company reports

- Wholly owned UAE subsidiary with a plant at Industrial Area, Umm Al Quwain ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.136). Subsidiary since 7 December 2022 (p.414).
- Described as an export-focused premium manufacturing hub serving 36+ countries (p.33, p.68). Management plans to expand it to 0.5 Mn ton (p.20).
- Audited by another (component) auditor. FY26: total assets ₹875.22 cr, revenue ₹1,728.15 cr, net cash outflow ₹49.60 cr (p.334, p.337).
- Q3 FY26 (limited review by another auditor): revenue ₹455.79 cr, PAT ₹21.47 cr; 9M FY26 revenue ₹1,237.77 cr, PAT ₹61.51 cr ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-results]], p.3). The filing does not name the subsidiary. It is matched to the UAE LLC by size and by the annual report's statement that the UAE LLC is the one audited by a component auditor (our inference).
- The CMD put Dubai volume at ~25,000 ton a month in December 2025, and said Dubai would "easily reach INR7,000" EBITDA/ton from Q4 FY26 ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-concall]], p.7).
- May 2026: "Our Dubai operations are operating at 40% utilization right now because of the on-going crisis there"; volume is lower but margins are said to be better ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]], p.4–5).
- CEO: Utkarsh Dwivedi (salary ₹3.50 cr) (p.135, p.400).

> [!warning] Contradiction
> The MD&A says the UAE facility reached "utilisation levels above 85%" (p.148). The Q4 FY26 commentary says "Dubai operated at low utilisation" because of the Middle East conflict (p.54). These may describe different periods, but the report does not reconcile them. See [[open-questions]].
- **Q1 FY27:**
  - UAE volume 25,929 ton (Q4 FY26 49,835; Q1 FY26 48,162) ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-presentation]], p.12).
  - Revenue ₹289.01 cr, PAT ₹1.62 cr (subsidiary reviewed by another auditor; our match to the UAE LLC) ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-results]], p.5). The Q1 filing reads ₹7.62 cr, but the Q2 filing's H1 PAT (₹28.48 cr) less Q2 PAT (₹26.86 cr) gives ₹1.62 cr ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.7); the 7.62 was probably an OCR error.
  - Management says volume fell to 5,000–6,000 ton a month because "both incoming and outgoing were closed", with pricing hits and high demurrage on ships. About 70,000 ton is in transit to the plant. July was 10,000–12,000 ton, with 24,000–25,000 ton a month targeted by September ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.5–6).
  - EBITDA is put at ₹5,000–6,000/ton "if operations ramp up" ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.17), against "easily reach INR7,000" said in January.
- **Q2 FY27:**
  - UAE volume 26,227 ton (Q2 FY26 57,931) ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-presentation]], p.13).
  - Subsidiary reviewed by another auditor: revenue ₹425.01 cr, PAT ₹26.86 cr; H1 revenue ₹714.02 cr, PAT ₹28.48 cr; total assets ₹1,090.93 cr ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.7).
- Management (October): the run rate is ~100,000 ton a year. Capacity rises from ~0.3 to 0.5 Mn ton by March 2027 with two new mills, for "fairly high-margin products" at EBITDA of ₹8,000–10,000/ton. The CMD: "it will be ready as a Rs 10,000-per-tonne EBITDA business, but right now it is out of my hands" ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 29:36, 41:52, 45:33).
- Per the Q2 call summaries: UAE running at "around 40% of their normal operating levels". Management describes it as "a potential ₹500 crore EBITDA business when fully operational". FY27 EBITDA could have been ~₹2,400 cr rather than ~₹2,200 cr had the UAE run normally ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results-note]], p.4, p.6; [[src-2026-10-axis-q2-fy27-note]], p.2).

## Our read

Revenue of ₹1,728 cr ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.337) is about 7.5% of group revenue (our calc.). Middle East conflict risk is concentrated here (see [[risks]]).
- Q1 PAT of ₹1.6 cr on ₹289 cr of revenue (0.6%) is far below the 9M FY26 run rate (₹61.5 cr on ₹1,238 cr, 5.0%) (our calculation). The UAE risk has fully materialised for now.
- Q2 revenue of ₹425 cr on 26,227 ton is ~₹1.62 lakh/ton, double the FY26 rate (our calc.). Volume did not recover (≈8,700 ton a month against the 24,000–25,000 targeted for September), so the revenue and profit jump probably came from something other than tube volume, such as selling the in-transit material. See [[open-questions]].
- The EBITDA claim has gone from ₹7,000 (January) to ₹5,000–6,000 (August) to ₹8,000–10,000 (October).
