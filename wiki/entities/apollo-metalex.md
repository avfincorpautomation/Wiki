# Apollo Metalex Limited (formerly Apollo Metalex Private Limited)

## What the company reports

- Subsidiary with two units at UPSIDC Industrial Area, Sikandrabad, Bulandshahr, UP ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.136, p.350).
- Jointly audited with [[apl-apollo-building-products]]. Combined FY26 figures: total assets ₹4,105.49 cr, revenue ₹8,160.26 cr, PAT ₹573.01 cr (p.337). A separate figure for Metalex alone was not extracted.
- Sunil Mittal is listed as CFO of Apollo Metalex (p.399).
- **1 August 2026:** the AML Board approved moving production from its **A-25 unit at Sikandrabad** to other group plants in phases, and the "consequential disposal of the land and building of the A-25 Unit", subject to approvals. The stated aims are better utilisation, lower operating and maintenance costs, and redeploying capital to core manufacturing. The filing says there will be "no adverse impact" on group capacity or volumes ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-results]], p.2).
- **Q2 FY27:** AML has signed an agreement to sell the A-25 land and building to a third party. Completion conditions were not met at quarter-end, so ₹15.59 cr is classified as held for sale and no gain is recognised ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.12).

## Our read

Sikandrabad is described as the hub for high-volume standard products in North India (p.68). Metalex's own margins are not visible from this source.
- A-25 is presumably one of the two Sikandrabad units listed in the annual report (our inference). The land sale value and buyer are not disclosed. Watch for a related-party buyer, as with [[blue-ocean-projects]]. See [[open-questions]].
