# Blue Ocean Projects Private Limited (BOPPL)

## What the company reports

- Wholly owned subsidiary "engaged in holding real estate assets"; subsidiary since 14 January 2017 ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.153, p.414).
- On 2 May 2026 the Board gave in-principle approval to divest the company's stake, "with the objective of unlocking capital and redeploying the same into the core manufacturing business". The valuation, buyer and structure are to be brought back to the Board "in due course" (p.153).
- Audit fee paid to the statutory auditor: ₹0.035 cr (p.180).
- The same approval was disclosed in the 2 May 2026 Board outcome filing. It describes the move as part of an "internal review of the Company's asset portfolio and group structure" ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-results]], p.1–2).
- **Sold.** The share purchase agreement was signed in Q1 FY27; the sale completed in Q2 FY27. Consideration ₹160.00 cr against a carrying amount of ₹150.30 cr. De-recognised: PPE ₹97.78 cr, right-of-use assets ₹21.50 cr and net current assets ₹31.02 cr. Gain of ₹9.70 cr booked in other income ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.12; [[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-results]], p.8).
- The buyer is not named in either filing.

## Our read

This is small in group terms, but the buyer's identity matters: a sale to a promoter-linked party would be a related-party transaction. Track it in [[guidance-tracker]].
- The 6% premium to book is modest for real estate held since 2017. Without the buyer's identity, a related-party sale cannot be ruled out. See [[open-questions]].
