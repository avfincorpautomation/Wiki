# Chetan Khandelwal — Chief Financial Officer

## What the company reports

- **Role:** Chief Financial Officer and key managerial personnel ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.154, p.398). With the CMD, he signs the CEO/CFO certification on financial reporting and internal controls (p.182).
- **Joined:** 30 October 2023. A chartered accountant with "over 21 years" of experience, including Head of Banking & Group Treasury Operations at Jindal Stainless Group, CFO of Jindal Coke Limited, and time at GHCL Limited (p.174).
- **Pay:** salary ₹1.80 cr in FY26 (₹1.28 cr in FY25) in the related-party note (p.400). The Board's Report gives his increase as 9% (p.158).
- Left the Q1 FY27 call for "an urgent meeting" ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.3).
- On the Q2 FY27 call, explained that the FDs of more than one year "are now reclassified as within one year" ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 08:37; low-confidence transcript).

> [!warning] Contradiction
