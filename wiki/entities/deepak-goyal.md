# Deepak Goyal (statutory name "Deepak Kumar") — Director (Operations) & Group CFO

## What the company reports

- **Role:** Director (Operations) and Group CFO; whole-time director. DIN 03056481 ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.130, p.135–136).
- **Background:** joined in 2013; previously with Surya Roshni. He is also **non-executive chairperson of SG Finserve Limited**, a listed company (p.130). We understand it to be linked to the promoter group, but the ingested sources do not say so; it is not in the annual report's related-party list (p.398). See [[promoter-group-companies]].
- **Pay:** salary ₹3.67 cr in FY26 (₹1.84 cr in FY25) in the related-party note (p.400). The governance report shows remuneration of ₹3.58 cr (p.171), a small difference presumably due to scope. His pay rose 9%, and the ratio of his pay to the median employee's is 44:1 (p.158).
- Quoted: "We are debt-free today, but we want to be liability-free." (p.30)
- Attended the Q1 FY27 call ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.3).

> [!warning] Contradiction
> His name is "Deepak Goyal" in the corporate pages (p.30, p.130, p.135) and on the calls, but "Deepak Kumar" (same DIN) in the statutory sections and the related-party note (p.136, p.154, p.171, p.398). Probably the same person. This is C10 in [[open-questions]].
>
> His pay also does not reconcile: salary nearly doubled from ₹1.84 cr to ₹3.67 cr (p.400), while the Board's Report gives his increase as 9% (p.158). The definitions may differ (for example, fixed pay against total including variable or share-based pay). See [[open-questions]].

## Our read

- He sits on both sides of the group: whole-time director of APL Apollo and chair of SG Finserve. That is relevant to the shared-services arrangement ([[group-shared-services-company]]), which will serve SG Finserv among others.
- His "liability-free" line is the promise that the H1 FY27 doubling of gross debt now tests ([[guidance-tracker]]).
