# Group Shared Services Company (SSC) — SG Enterprise Solutions Pvt Ltd

## What the company reports

- On 1 August 2026 the Board approved APL Apollo's participation in the proposed incorporation of a Group Shared Services Company. APL Apollo will subscribe to or acquire **up to 20%** of its equity, for **no more than ₹1 crore**. On incorporation the SSC is expected to be an **associate** of the company. The Finance Committee is authorised to complete it ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-results]], p.1).
- Purpose: to provide "common corporate support services to participating Group entities through a centralised shared services model" (p.1).
- Management says the SSC will handle HR, IT and branding across "SG Finserv, SG Mart and APL Apollo as well as Apollo Pipes", to cut duplicated costs ("every company has a separate HR head, every company has a separate IT head") — Sanjay Gupta ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.15).
- The August filing did not name the other shareholders, the service-fee basis, or the services the SSC will supply to APL Apollo.
- **Identified (October 2026):** the SSC is **SG Enterprise Solutions Private Limited**, incorporated 10 September 2026, with 50,000 equity shares of ₹10. It has not started operations ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.3–4).
- On 26 October 2026 the Board approved buying a further 31% (15,500 shares) for ₹1,55,000 in cash, taking APL Apollo from 20% to **51%**. The SSC becomes a **subsidiary**; completion is expected by 31 December 2026 (p.1, p.4).
- **Sellers:** S Gupta Holding Pvt Ltd (7,750 shares), which is promoter group, and SG Green Buildings Pvt Ltd (7,750 shares), in which "the promoters of the Company, together with their immediate relatives, hold more than 20%". The filing classes the deal as a related-party transaction "on an arm's length basis" (p.3).
- It is currently consolidated as an associate with nil share of profit ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.7–8).

## Our read

- *August view, now superseded by the 51% plan:* APL Apollo will hold a minority stake (≤20%), so the other ~80% sits outside the listed company. The CMD names the participating firms as SG Finserv, SG Mart and Apollo Pipes. We understand these to be companies linked to the Gupta promoter family; this wiki has no source confirming their ownership yet.
- The equity cheque is trivial. What matters is the **service fees APL Apollo will pay** into a vehicle it does not control. This should appear in related-party transactions from FY27. Watch the amount and the pricing basis, alongside [[sg-green-logistics]] (₹67 cr in FY26).
- See [[governance]] and [[open-questions]].
- **October update:** the founding shareholders are now known to be promoter entities. APL Apollo's 51% control means the costs are consolidated. But fees billed to SG Mart, SG Finserv and Apollo Pipes will flow through an APL Apollo subsidiary, so related-party disclosures are still needed. Who holds the remaining 49% after completion is not stated.
