# ICRA Limited (credit rating agency)

**What it is:** an Indian credit rating agency, set up in 1991, listed on BSE/NSE. Moody's Investors Service is its largest shareholder ([[src-2026-02-icra-rating]], p.8). It rates APL Apollo's bank facilities and commercial paper.

## What the company reports

- **Current rating (26 February 2026):** [ICRA]AA+ (Stable) long-term / [ICRA]A1+ short-term, on ₹4,220.34 cr of rated limits. Commercial paper ₹1,200 cr rated A1+ (p.1).
- **History** (p.5):
  - AA (Stable): June 2022 – March 2023.
  - AA (Positive): December 2023 – March 2024.
  - **Upgraded to AA+ (Stable): September 2024.**
  - Reaffirmed: November 2024, June 2025, February 2026.
- **Downgrade trigger:** sustained earnings deterioration, or debt-funded capex, investment or acquisition taking total debt/OPBITDA **above 0.75×** on a sustained basis (p.3).
- **ICRA's view:**
  - Expected OPBITDA/ton ₹4,500–5,000 over the medium term (p.1).
  - Capacity ~6.8 MTPA by FY2028 (p.1).
  - Debt "expected to decrease further" (p.2).
- The annual report also lists credit ratings; see [[governance]].

## Our read

- **Distance from the trigger:** at 30 September 2026, gross debt ≈₹918 cr on TTM EBITDA of ~₹1,972 cr is ~0.47× (our calculation from [[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.10 and [[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results-note]], p.1). That is below 0.75×, but up from ~0.25× at FY26 end. ICRA expected debt to fall; it doubled.
- **Limit mix:** the February 2026 shift from non-fund-based limits (₹1,000 cr → nil) to cash credit (₹1,460 → ₹2,975 cr) is worth matching against the Q2 FY27 rise in current borrowings. See [[supplier-finance]].
- **Independent benchmark:** ICRA's capacity (6.8 vs 8 MTPA) and margin (₹4,500–5,000 vs ₹6,000) expectations are a more conservative check on management's targets. See [[guidance-tracker]].
