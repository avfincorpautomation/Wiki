**Other group-linked companies mentioned:**
- **SG Finserve Limited** (listed): [[deepak-goyal]] is its non-executive chairperson (p.130). Several APL Apollo independent directors are also independent directors of SG Finserve (p.166). It is one of the companies the [[group-shared-services-company]] will serve ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.15).
- **SG Mart Limited** (listed): an APL Apollo independent director is also an independent director of SG Mart (p.166). It will also be served by the shared-services company ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.15).
- **Apollo Pipes Limited:** a director of APL Apollo is a non-executive director of Apollo Pipes; the extracted table does not make clear which one (p.166). It will also be served by the shared-services company ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.15).
- **[[group-shared-services-company]]** (SG Enterprise Solutions Pvt Ltd): to become a 51% APL Apollo subsidiary, with the extra shares bought from S Gupta Holding and SG Green Buildings ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.1, p.3).

## Our read

- **Small amounts, but rising.** Apart from SG Green Logistics (₹67 cr, tripled) and the new ₹10 cr payment to the sports company, dealings are small. The direction is towards more links: a shared-services company spanning the group's listed companies, and board seats held across SG Finserve and SG Mart.
- **Independence.** APL Apollo independent directors who also sit on SG Finserve and SG Mart boards are worth noting when the board approves related-party transactions.
- **Gaps.** The ingested sources do not set out who owns SG Finserve, SG Mart and Apollo Pipes. We understand them to be Gupta-family companies; this needs a source (their shareholding filings). See [[open-questions]].
