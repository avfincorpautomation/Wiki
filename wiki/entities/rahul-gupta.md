# Rahul Gupta — Director (son of the CMD)

## What the company reports

- **Role:** board director, classed **non-executive** in the corporate governance report (p.165). Son of CMD [[sanjay-gupta]] ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.166, p.398).
- **Responsibilities** as described in the director profiles: operations, marketing and institutional sales (p.130).
- **Pay:** salary of **₹6.93 cr in FY26 (₹3.96 cr in FY25)**, shown in the related-party note (p.400). The governance report shows no or nominal sitting fees for him (p.171).
- Quoted: "Being a dominant brand, our pricing power is strong." (p.40)
- He and the CFO left the Q1 FY27 call for "an urgent meeting" ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.3).

> [!warning] Contradiction
> He is classed as a non-executive director, and the governance report says there is no pecuniary relationship with non-executive directors beyond sitting fees (p.165, p.171). Yet the related-party note shows a salary of ₹6.93 cr (p.400), up 75% on FY25. This is C9 in [[open-questions]].

## Our read

- His FY26 salary is above the CMD's (nil in FY26) and above the whole-time director Deepak Kumar's (₹3.67 cr) (p.400). Together with Vinay Gupta's ₹6.82 cr (p.400; Vinay Gupta is listed as "Managing Director" in the related-party list on p.398 but as "Director" elsewhere), promoter-family pay is a governance item to track.
