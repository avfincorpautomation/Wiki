# S Gupta Holding Private Limited (formerly APL Infrastructure Private Limited)

## What the company reports

- Main promoter holding company: 7,38,60,062 shares, 26.61% of APL Apollo Tubes at 31 March 2026 and 31 March 2025 ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.375).
- Total promoter and promoter group holding: 28.26% (p.375).
- Listed as a related party (enterprise significantly influenced by KMP and relatives). Rent paid to it was nil in FY26 (FY25: ₹0.16 cr) (p.399–400).
- Other promoter-linked related parties named in the note: Apollo Pipes Ltd, Apollo Coated Products Pvt Ltd, [[sg-green-logistics]], SG Ornate Solar Solutions Pvt Ltd, SG Green Building Pvt Ltd, S Gupta Sports and Entertainment Pvt Ltd (p.399).
- October 2026: S Gupta Holding will sell 7,750 shares of SG Enterprise Solutions (the shared-services company) to APL Apollo at par, as part of the 31% stake purchase. The filing calls it "a promoter group of the Company" ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.3). See [[group-shared-services-company]].
