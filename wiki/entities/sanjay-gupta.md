# Sanjay Gupta

## What the company reports

- Promoter, Chairman & Managing Director (DIN 00233188); about 29 years in the steel industry; son of Sudesh Gupta ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.129, p.136).
- Brother of director Vinay Gupta and father of director [[rahul-gupta]]; Rohan Gupta (son) is also a related party (p.166, p.399).
- Remuneration from the company: nil in FY26 (FY25: ₹4.39 cr) (p.171, p.400). Ratio to median employee pay: nil (p.158).
- Holds 70 shares directly; the family's holding is mainly through [[s-gupta-holding]] (26.61%) (p.375).
- Has personally guaranteed (with Vinay Gupta) some bank credit facilities (p.378).
- Signed the FY26 Chairman's letter, which set the FY27 guidance (p.22). See [[guidance-tracker]].

## Our read

He controls the strategy and the main promoter vehicle. Taking no salary in FY26 is shareholder-friendly on its face, but family members' pay rose (see [[governance]]).
