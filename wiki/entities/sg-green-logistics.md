# SG Green Logistics Private Limited

## What the company reports

- Related party: an "enterprise significantly influenced by KMP and their relatives" ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.399).
- Purchase of services by the group: ₹67.33 cr in FY26 vs ₹21.20 cr in FY25 (p.399).
- Other transactions: expenses incurred on its behalf ₹3.47 cr in FY26; sale of scrap ₹0.01 cr; sale of PPE to it ₹0.22 cr in FY25 (p.399–400).

## Our read

The logistics spend more than tripled while volume grew 11%. That may reflect a deliberate shift of freight to this entity. The pricing basis is not disclosed beyond the standard "arm's length" statement (p.155). Ask management.
