# Source: Q3 FY26 earnings conference call transcript

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** transcript of the analyst/investor call hosted by Ambit Capital.
- **Date of call:** 22 January 2026; transcript filed 27 January 2026.
- **Period discussed:** Q3 FY26 and 9M FY26, with guidance for Q4 FY26, FY27 and FY28.
- **Management on the call:** [[sanjay-gupta]] (CMD), Rahul Gupta (Director), Deepak Goyal (Director – Operations), Anubhav Gupta (CSO), Chetan Khandelwal (CFO).
- **File:** `raw/transcripts/2026-01-q3-fy26-concall.pdf` (19 PDF pages). Page numbers below are PDF pages.
- **Quality note:** several of the CMD's answers are machine-translated from Hindi and are hard to follow. Speaker attribution in the extracted text is sometimes misaligned. Where a quote's speaker is unclear, it is attributed to "management". **Medium confidence.**

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Guidance raised:** "we are upgrading our sales volume growth guidance of 20% for 4QFY26 and FY27, with the EBITDA guidance of almost INR5,500 per ton" — Anubhav Gupta (p.3). This was repeated: "20% volume growth for Q4 FY '26 and for the full year FY '27 over FY '26" (p.6).
- **Specific numbers given:** FY26 around 3.55 Mn ton, with Q4 at 10–10.5 lakh ton (p.5); FY27 "Minimum 4.2 million ton" and EBITDA/ton "Yes, minimum" ₹5,500 (p.11); FY27 ROCE "could hit 40%" (p.11); FY28 volume growth to be maintained at 20% (p.15); FY26 EBITDA "We are targeting that we should cross INR1,900 crores" (p.8).
- **The CMD on guidance credibility:** after earlier cuts, "we are not ready to take back the guidelines at any cost" (p.9).
- **Capacity:** 5 → 8 Mn ton for about ₹1,500 cr, funded internally over two years: ₹1,300 cr for four greenfield plants (Gorakhpur, Siliguri, New Malur, Bhuj) plus a Raipur brownfield, and ₹200 cr for 1 Mn ton of debottlenecking (p.4, p.10). Exit capacity about 6–6.25 Mn ton at FY27 end; bulk of greenfield from Q1 FY28 (p.15). Bhuj is for exports (p.10).
- **Super-speciality raised from 1 to 2 Mn ton**, with target EBITDA above ₹10,000/ton via JVs; more clarity "in the next 2 to 3 quarters" (p.10–11).
- **Brand pricing:** 12 months after the price increase, a ₹3,000–4,000/ton premium for APL Apollo-branded products "is now the new normal" (p.3).
- **SG Premium economics:** EBITDA ₹1,500–2,000/ton; 60,000–70,000 ton in Q3; "under 10%" of FY27 volume; "We will sell SG premium no matter what the volume is, whatever the price. Maybe price is less than HR coil" (p.7, p.16, p.18). See [[sg-premium]].
- **Dividend:** "right now, we are going with minimum 20% of dividend payout policy. We increased to 25% minimum." — Sanjay Gupta (p.12).
- **Liability-free target:** with a strong Q4, surplus cash "could be INR1,500 crores … to match our current liabilities, so that we can be a liability-free company" (p.4).

## 5. What changed since the previous source of this type

This is the first concall ingested. Management referred to the Q1 FY26 call's guidance of 10–15% volume growth and EBITDA/ton of ₹4,800–5,000 (p.3, p.5). Both were raised on this call.

**Checked against later sources:** Q4 FY26 volume was 925 thousand ton (+9%), FY26 volume 3.49 Mn ton and FY26 EBITDA ~₹1,802 cr ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.24, p.25, p.54). The Q4 volume, FY26 volume and ₹1,900 cr EBITDA guidance were missed. In May 2026 the FY27 guidance was restated as volume +15–20% and EBITDA/ton ₹5,300–5,700 ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.20, p.22). See [[guidance-tracker]].
