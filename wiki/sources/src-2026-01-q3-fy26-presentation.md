# Source: Q3 FY26 earnings presentation and press release

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** investor presentation plus press release.
- **Date:** filed 22 January 2026.
- **Period:** Q3 FY26 (October–December 2025) and 9M FY26.
- **File:** `raw/presentations/2026-01-q3-fy26-investor-presentation.pdf` (39 PDF pages; press release on p.37–38).
- Page numbers below are PDF pages.

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Record quarter:** volume 917 thousand ton (+11% YoY, +7% QoQ); revenue ₹58.2 Bn; EBITDA ₹4.7 Bn (+37% YoY); EBITDA/ton ₹5,146 (+23% YoY, −2% QoQ); net profit ₹3.1 Bn (+43% YoY) (p.5).
- **9M FY26:** volume 2,566 thousand ton (+11%); EBITDA ₹12.9 Bn (+64%); EBITDA/ton ₹5,030; net profit ₹8.5 Bn (+83%) (p.6).
- **First disclosure of EBITDA/ton by product line** (p.10–11): Heavy ₹8,767; Light ₹6,023; General (includes SG Premium) ₹3,149; Rust-proof ₹6,324; Coated ₹7,011; Galv agri/industrial ₹5,398 (Q3 FY26). See [[value-added-products]].
- **Capacity plan in detail** (p.12): 2 Mn ton greenfield/brownfield (Gorakhpur 0.2, Siliguri 0.3, Bhuj 0.3, New Malur 0.6, Raipur 0.6 Mn ton), capex ₹13 Bn by FY28; 1 Mn ton debottlenecking, capex ₹2 Bn by FY28; a further 2 Mn ton "future capacity", with the plan to be shared within a year; 10 Mn ton by FY30. See [[capital-allocation]].
- **December 2025 sales ~370 thousand ton**, an annualised 4.4 Mn ton, or ~89% of 5 Mn ton capacity (p.12).
- **Net cash ₹5.6 Bn at 9M FY26**, but this classes ₹6.1 Bn of fixed deposits as cash, while the accounts carry them in other financial assets (p.20).
- **Financial priorities slide:** "Achieve ROCE 35%" and "Net cash company" (p.9).
- ESG slides still show "Scope 1 & 2 emissions reduction by 25% by 2030" (vs FY22) and list "Attrition rate below 5%" and "Zero accidents" as achievements (p.29–31).
