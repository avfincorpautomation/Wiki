# Source: Q3 FY26 unaudited financial results (standalone and consolidated)

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** Regulation 33 filing — unaudited results for the quarter and nine months to 31 December 2025, with limited review reports by [[walker-chandiok]].
- **Date:** Board meeting and filing 22 January 2026.
- **Period:** Q3 FY26 and 9M FY26.
- **File:** `raw/filings/2026-01-q3-fy26-results.pdf` (11 PDF pages).
- **Quality note:** the results tables are a scanned image, and the text came out partly garbled. Figures below were checked against the presentation ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-presentation]]) and the annual report ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]]). Line items that could not be read reliably are left out. **Medium confidence.**

## 2. Key takeaways

- Consolidated total revenue from operations ₹5,815.13 cr in Q3 FY26 (Q2: ₹5,206.30 cr; Q3 FY25: ₹5,432.73 cr) (p.5).
- Consolidated PBT ₹404.47 cr, tax ₹94.43 cr, PAT ₹310.04 cr (Q3 FY25 PAT: ₹216.97 cr; Q2 FY26: ₹301.54 cr) (p.5).
- Basic EPS ₹11.17 for the quarter, ₹30.58 for 9M FY26 (9M FY25: ₹16.72) (p.5).
- The limited review is unmodified. One subsidiary was reviewed by another auditor: revenue ₹455.79 cr and PAT ₹21.47 cr in Q3; ₹1,237.77 cr and ₹61.51 cr for 9M (p.3). By its size this matches the UAE subsidiary [[apl-apollo-tubes-company-llc]] (our inference).
- New Labour Codes took effect 21 November 2025. The company has booked an incremental liability it calls "not material" (p.6).
- Net worth (consolidated) ₹4,911.39 cr at 31 December 2025 (p.6).
- One reportable segment (p.6).
