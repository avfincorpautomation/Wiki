# Source: ICRA rating rationale — ratings reaffirmed, rated amount enhanced

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** credit rating rationale by [[icra]] (Moody's-affiliated Indian rating agency).
- **Date:** 26 February 2026. **Period covered:** FY2024–FY2025 audited figures, 9M FY2026, and the outlook to FY2028.
- **File:** `raw/filings/2026-02-credit-rating-icra.pdf` (10 PDF pages; p.10 is blank). Page numbers below are PDF pages, matching ICRA's printed "Page |n".
- **Scope:** a consolidated view of APL Apollo Tubes and its 100% subsidiaries: Apollo Metalex, Blue Ocean Projects, APL Apollo Building Products, APL Apollo Mart and the UAE LLC (p.1, p.7).

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Ratings reaffirmed:** [ICRA]AA+ (Stable) long-term and [ICRA]A1+ short-term. Commercial paper is rated A1+ (p.1).
- **Facility mix changed** (p.1, p.7):
  - Total rated limits rose from ₹3,720.34 cr to ₹4,220.34 cr.
  - **Cash credit (fund-based) rose from ₹1,460 cr to ₹2,975 cr.**
  - **Short-term non-fund-based limits fell from ₹1,000 cr to nil.**
  - The term loan went from ₹24.15 cr to nil; commercial paper is unchanged at ₹1,200 cr; unallocated limits are ₹45.34 cr.
- **ICRA's own expectations are more conservative than management's:**
  - Medium-term OPBITDA/ton of **₹4,500–5,000** (p.1).
  - Capacity of **~6.8 MTPA by FY2028**, including Raipur, Gorakhpur, "Kolkata" (West Bengal) and Bhuj (p.1).
  - Capex of ~₹500 cr a year, with "no major debt funded capex plans" (p.1, p.3).
- **Credit metrics expected for FY2026:** interest cover ~15× and total debt/OPBITDA below 0.5× (p.1–2). Interest cover was ~13.8× in 9M FY2026 and 9× in FY2025 (p.2).
- **Downgrade trigger:** a "sustained deterioration in earnings", or debt-funded capex, investment or acquisition that lifts total debt/OPBITDA **above 0.75×** on a sustained basis (p.3).
- **Strengths cited** (p.2):
  - Leadership in ERW pipes and 800+ dealers.
  - Receivable days under 10 (from ~30, after a shift to cash-and-carry).
  - Inventory under 40 days.
  - Net working capital at ~0–3% of operating income.
- **Challenges cited:** raw-material price lag, and intense competition with low entry barriers. Competition "moderates the Group's pricing power" (p.2).
- **Liquidity is "Strong":** cash and liquid investments of ~₹613 cr at 30 September 2025, plus undrawn limits. Scheduled repayments are ~₹157 cr in FY2026 and ₹148 cr in FY2027 (p.3).
