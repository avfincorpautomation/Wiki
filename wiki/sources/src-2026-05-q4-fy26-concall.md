# Source: Q4 FY26 earnings conference call transcript

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** transcript of the analyst/investor call hosted by Emkay Global.
- **Date of call:** 4 May 2026; transcript filed 6 May 2026.
- **Period discussed:** Q4 FY26 and FY26, with commentary on April–June 2026 and FY27.
- **Management on the call:** [[sanjay-gupta]] (CMD), Rahul Gupta, Deepak Goyal, Anubhav Gupta (CSO), Chetan Khandelwal (CFO).

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Why the January guidance was missed (management's account):** "Things looked so good till 28th of February and then the Middle East crisis started" (p.3). The factors cited were a shortage of steel from Indian mills, Dubai running at 40% utilisation, destocking by channel partners who fear a steel price correction, the energy crisis in March, and labour shortages from heat and elections (p.4). "If war had not started, we would have met our guidance which we had given in the quarter three" (p.10).
- **Gas shortage:** rust-proof and coated product lines had temporary shutdowns of 10–15 days in March and moved to alternate fuels. Plants are running at 80–90% because of the "fear factor" (p.5, p.14).
- **FY27 guidance kept at 15–20% volume / 20–25% EBITDA / 25–30% PAT** (p.8, p.11), but with qualifications: "Volume. I think 20% is difficult. If it gets worse than we targeting 15%" — Sanjay Gupta (p.8). "We are not reducing our guidance as of now, right, for the absolute EBITDA" — Anubhav Gupta (p.12).
- **Near-term volume:** April 2026 was 2.5 lakh ton; May 2.75–3 lakh and June 3–3.5 lakh targeted, for a Q1 FY27 total of about 8.75 lakh ton against 7.92 lakh in Q1 FY26 (p.11–12).
- **Margin first:** "We are focusing on our margin. When the atmosphere gets a little better … we will pull back our volume by adjusting the margin a little" (p.5). EBITDA/ton of ₹5,000–5,500 is called sustainable "on a long term basis", with ₹6,000+ being attempted now (p.6).
- **Margin drivers:** the January 2025 price increase of "almost INR1,500 per ton" on the Apollo general segment "is straightaway coming to our EBITDA", taking General from ~₹2,000 to ₹3,500+/ton (p.15). Steel shortages lifted galvanised and coated margins (p.5). Management says inventory gains were minimal because inventory churns in under 30 days (p.6, p.16).
- **Cash:** net cash rose from ~₹550 cr (Dec) to ~₹1,510 cr (Mar). Reasons given: inventory down 30,000–40,000 ton (~₹250 cr by value), "better payment terms from the creditors", and ~₹350 cr of quarterly profit (p.7–8).
- **Shareholder returns:** "I have a liability of INR500 crores. Once I eliminate this liability in Q1 and Q2, I'm unsure what to do with the cash. Either increase the dividend or do a buyback." Asked whether to expect a good dividend this year: "I can say yes." — Sanjay Gupta (p.7).
- **Capex:** "around INR500 crores, INR600 crores yearly capex … total pending plan … around INR1,400 crores, INR1,500 crores … We will complete it in the next 2 and 2.5 years for 8 million tons" — Sanjay Gupta (p.6). Earlier in the same call: the 8 Mn ton by FY28 plan "remains totally on-track" (p.4).

## 5. What changed since the previous source of this type

Compared with [[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-concall]]:
- **Tone** moved from volume-led ("20% volume growth … minimum 4.2 million ton") to margin-led ("we are not focusing on our volume").
- **January guidance described differently.** The CSO now describes the Q3 guidance as "near about 1 million ton … with INR5,300-INR5,500 per ton" (p.10). The Q3 transcript has ₹5,500 "minimum" and a firm 20% growth.
- **Market share history changed:** "65% … for almost 4 years" in January vs "improved to 65% from 55%" in FY26 now (p.13).
- **Capex figures largely unchanged** (₹1,400–1,500 cr vs ₹1,500 cr), but the completion window is now "2 and 2.5 years".
- **New this call:** the buyback/dividend option is tied to Q1–Q2 FY27, and SG Premium is put at 8–9% of volume.
