# Source: Q4 FY26 earnings presentation and press release

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** investor presentation.
- **Date:** filed 2 May 2026.
- **Period:** Q4 FY26 (January–March 2026) and FY26.
- **File:** `raw/presentations/2026-05-q4-fy26-investor-presentation.pdf` (38 PDF pages). Page numbers below are PDF pages.

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Q4 FY26:** volume 925 thousand ton (+9% YoY, +1% QoQ); revenue ₹62.7 Bn (+14%); EBITDA ₹5.1 Bn (+24%); EBITDA/ton ₹5,525, a record (+14% YoY, +7% QoQ); net profit ₹3.5 Bn (+21%); value-added share 55% (Q3: 57%) (p.6).
- **FY26:** volume 3,491 thousand ton; revenue ₹230.8 Bn; EBITDA ₹18.0 Bn; EBITDA/ton ₹5,161; net profit ₹12.0 Bn; ROCE 37.3%; ROE 25.3%; net cash ₹15.3 Bn (p.7).
- **Product lines, FY26:** General (incl. [[sg-premium]]) ₹3,206/ton on 1,482 thousand ton (FY25: ₹1,705); Heavy ₹8,733; Rust-proof ₹6,320; Coated ₹6,951 (p.11). Q4 alone: General ₹3,405, Rust-proof ₹7,261, Coated ₹8,047, Heavy ₹9,010 (p.10).
- **Capacity slide simplified:** 5 → 8 Mn ton by FY28 = 2 Mn ton greenfield/brownfield ("East (Gorakhpur, Siliguri)", "South (Malur)", "Western coastal area") + 1 Mn ton debottlenecking. Plant tonnages, the Raipur brownfield line and capex figures shown in Q3 are **no longer shown** (p.12; compare [[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-presentation]], p.12).
- **Net cash ₹15.3 Bn** counts ₹11 Bn of fixed deposits as cash; the accounts carry them in other financial assets (p.20). Cash bridge: FY25 net cash ₹3,047 Mn + OCF ₹20,023 Mn − capex ₹6,520 Mn + investments ₹1,026 Mn − dividend and others ₹2,253 Mn = ₹15,323 Mn (p.18).
- **Free cash flow ₹13.4 Bn** in FY26 (p.17).
- **Revenue regrouping:** "Re-grouping has been done for the sale of traded products of Rs 6.19 Bn in the previous quarters of FY26 (Q1FY26, Q2FY26, Q3FY26)". Q4 trading revenue was ₹5.3 Bn (p.19). The quarterly revenues in the presentation (p.8) add up to ₹224.6 Bn, ₹6.2 Bn short of the annual ₹230.8 Bn (our calculation).
- **Market sizing changed:** FY26 structural tube market ~10 Mn ton (5.0 HRC-based + 5.0 sponge-iron-based), projected 18.3 Mn ton by FY31E (13.3 HRC-based) (p.23). The Q3 deck had 9 Mn ton in FY24 and 17.3 Mn ton by 2030.
- The ESG slides still say "Scope 1 & 2 emissions reduction by 25% by 2030" and list "Attrition rate below 5%" and "Zero accidents" (p.29–30). DJSI 91st percentile in 2025 (p.28).

## 5. What changed since the previous source of this type

Compared with [[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-presentation]]:
- Plant-level capacity detail, the ₹13 Bn + ₹2 Bn capex figures, the 10 Mn ton FY30 line and the "Execution Plan to be shared in 1 year" were all removed from the capacity slide (p.12).
- The ROCE history now matches the annual report (33.0 / 27.7 / 27.5 / 24.5). The Q3 deck showed 34.6 / 29.2 / 29.5 / 24.5 (p.15).
- FY22 PAT is shown as ₹6,190 Mn, against ₹5,573 Mn in the annual report KPI chart ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]], p.28).
- Market size rebased to FY26 = 10 Mn ton, with a 50/50 HRC vs sponge-iron split.
- The Q4 trading revenue disclosure and the regrouping note are new.
