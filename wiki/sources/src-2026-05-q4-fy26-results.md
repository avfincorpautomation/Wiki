# Source: Q4 FY26 and FY26 audited financial results (outcome of Board meeting, 2 May 2026)

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** Regulation 30/33 filing. It contains the outcome of the 2 May 2026 Board meeting, audited standalone and consolidated results for the quarter and year to 31 March 2026, the auditor's reports by [[walker-chandiok]], and disclosures on the subsidiary actions.
- **Date:** 2 May 2026.
- **File:** `raw/filings/2026-05-q4-fy26-results.pdf` (25 PDF pages).
- **Quality note:** the results tables are scanned and partly garbled. Full-year figures match the annual report ([[src-2026-08-annual-report-fy26]]). Quarterly figures were cross-checked against the presentation ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-presentation]]). **Medium confidence** on items not in either.

## 2. Key takeaways

- Q4 FY26 consolidated total revenue from operations ₹6,269.16 cr (Q4 FY25: ₹5,508.60 cr); PBT ₹456.61 cr; tax ₹102.26 cr; PAT ₹354.35 cr (Q4 FY25: ₹293.11 cr); basic EPS ₹12.76 (Q4 FY25: ₹10.56) (p.14).
- FY26 audited: revenue ₹23,079.00 cr, PAT ₹1,203.08 cr, EPS ₹43.34. Same as the annual report (p.14).
- The audit opinion is unmodified, standalone and consolidated (p.2).
- Final dividend of ₹8.50 per ₹2 share recommended (p.1).
- Second terms recommended for four independent directors (p.1).
- [[apl-apollo-mart]] liquidation: the company was incorporated in 2021 to trade and distribute steel, "has remained non-operational since incorporation" and has "only limited operations in the recent years". FY26 revenue ₹9.02 cr (0.04% of group); **net worth ₹111.72 cr (2.11% of group)** (p.6).
- In-principle approval to divest [[blue-ocean-projects]], "based on independent valuation" (p.2).
- Q4 figures are the balancing figure between the audited full year and the published 9M (p.17, note 2). Because ₹619 cr of traded-product sales were regrouped into Q1–Q3 ([[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-presentation]], p.19), the 9M revenue implied here (₹23,079.00 − ₹6,269.16 = ₹16,809.84 cr) is higher than the ₹16,197.20 cr first reported in January ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-results]], p.5) (our calculation).
