# Source: APL Apollo Tubes — Integrated Annual Report FY26 ("The Architecture of Returns")

- **Period covered:** FY26 (1 April 2025 – 31 March 2026).

- **Coverage note:** read in full — corporate overview pp.1–71, 73–76, 101–136; MD&A pp.137–150; Board's Report and annexures pp.151–165; consolidated auditor's report and primary statements pp.334–347; selected consolidated notes (pp.350, 362, 369, 375, 378, 386, 388, 392–393, 399–400, 410, 414–415). ## 2. Key takeaways

- **Record year; profit grew much faster than volume.** Volume 3.49 Mn ton (+11%), revenue ₹23,079 cr (+11.5%), PAT ₹1,203 cr (+59%), EBITDA/ton ₹5,161 (+36%) (p.15, p.151, p.344). See [[financials]], [[kpis]].
- **Returns stepped up:** ROCE 37.3% (FY25: 24.5%), ROE 25.3% (p.18, p.149). Management credits a price increase taken in January 2025, a richer value-added mix (58% of sales) and the SG Premium value brand (p.11–14, p.148). See [[business-model]].
- **Capacity plan: 5 → 8 Mn ton by FY28, 10 Mn ton by 2030**, said to be fully internally funded; 1 Mn ton from debottlenecking, new plants at Gorakhpur, Siliguri, New Malur and a West Coast export plant; final 2 Mn ton for "super-speciality" via unnamed foreign JVs (p.5, p.7, p.9, p.20, p.105).
- **FY27 guidance (Chairman):** volume +15–20%, EBITDA +20–25%, PAT +25–30% (p.22). Medium-term EBITDA/ton target ₹5,300–5,700 (p.20). See [[guidance-tracker]].
- **Balance sheet presented as "net cash ₹15.3 Bn"**, gross borrowings ₹452 cr (p.18, p.343). But ₹1,382 cr of trade payables are under a **supplier finance arrangement** where banks have already paid the suppliers (p.386). See [[supplier-finance]], [[capital-allocation]].
- **Dividend ₹8.50/share (₹236 cr, ~20% payout)**; payout floor raised to 20%; buybacks/higher dividends flagged once "permanent net-cash phase" starts in FY27 (p.21, p.410).
- **Market position claims:** APL Apollo brands' market share 65% (55% in FY22); 60–70% share in supply to PEB makers; exports ₹2,015 cr (p.24, p.39, p.152, p.388).
- **Governance items worth tracking:** auditor change (Deloitte → Walker Chandiok); key audit matter on ₹603 cr of single-vendor capex; promoter-linked logistics purchases tripled to ₹67 cr; non-executive promoter-family directors drew ~₹6.8–6.9 cr salaries each (p.334–337, p.399–400). See [[governance]].
- **Post-balance-sheet:** voluntary liquidation of APL Apollo Mart Ltd; in-principle approval to divest Blue Ocean Projects (real-estate holding subsidiary); grant of 30 lakh SARs on 1 April 2026 (p.153, p.155).
- **Several internal inconsistencies** in the report (attrition, safety, CSR, emissions target, dividend payout, dealer count) — logged in [[open-questions]].
