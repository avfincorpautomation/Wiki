# Source: Q1 FY27 earnings conference call transcript

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** transcript of the analyst/investor call hosted by Antique Stock Broking.
- **Date of call:** 3 August 2026; transcript filed 5 August 2026.
- **Period discussed:** Q1 FY27, July 2026, and the FY27–FY28 outlook.
- **Management on the call:** [[sanjay-gupta]] (CMD), Anubhav Gupta (CSO) and Deepak Goyal. Rahul Gupta and the CFO, Chetan Khandelwal, are listed but "have gone for an urgent meeting" (p.3).
- **File:** `raw/transcripts/2026-08-q1-fy27-concall.pdf` (20 PDF pages). Page numbers below are PDF pages; the printed "Page x of 18" is one lower.
- **Quality note:** the CMD's answers are translated from Hindi, and speaker labels are misaligned in places. Attributions follow the content. **Medium confidence.**

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Four reasons given for the volume miss** (CSO, p.3–4):
  1. UAE lost "almost 25,000 tons" QoQ.
  2. SG Premium volume fell because the gap with secondary steel was high.
  3. The energy crisis cost 25,000–30,000 ton of rust-proof and roofing volume.
  4. High inflation in construction materials led to channel destocking and delayed purchases by contractors.

  The Q1 run rate was ~250,000 ton a month.
- **Margin over volume:** "we chose to focus on profitability". Gross profit rose ~₹1,000/ton QoQ because prices were raised ₹100–200/ton more than steel costs (p.4).
- **Guidance reaffirmed, with a hedge:**
  - CSO: EBITDA/ton ₹5,000–5,500 "throughout the year" and 20% absolute EBITDA growth for FY27 (p.4).
  - CMD on volume: "I don't think I will have any problem to cross 15% volume growth. Yes, some tailwinds will be needed to cross 20%" (p.6).
  - Asked if guidance was maintained: "Yes. 101%" (p.5).
  - Later: volume 15–20%, EBITDA "More than 20%" (p.16).
- **Quarterly path given by the CMD:** Q2 ~10 lakh ton, Q3 ~10.5 lakh ton, Q4 ~12 lakh ton, "Our main game play will be Q4" (p.18). July was "3 lakh tons plus"; the targets are 3.25–3.35 lakh in August and 3.5–3.6 lakh in September (p.6, p.16).
- **Some prices have been cut:** "15% to 20% of our total basket, we have done some aggressive pricing policy" (p.16). "Currently, with the pricing policy, we have a hit" (p.18).
- **Product-line margin disclosure has stopped.** Asked to resume it: "With the competitors the more our margin was seen, we used to get a hit there. So technically we have finished it" (CMD, p.17).
- **New margin numbers by category** (CSO, p.17):
  - APL Apollo brand and Roofing: ₹6,000–7,000/ton targeted; Roofing "right now" is in that range.
  - SG Premium: ~₹500/ton. Earlier on the call: ₹0–1,000/ton (p.13).
  - UAE: ₹5,000–6,000/ton if it ramps up.
- **Patra gap:** the gap between primary and secondary steel is ₹10–12/kg. At that gap, "we don't have much of a play in it and there is no benefit in selling goods by losing cash". At ₹3–5/kg, SG Premium "becomes a masterstroke" (CMD, p.14). The CMD says the issue hits "our 30% business" and may last "another one, two, or three quarters" (p.7).
- **Capacity:**
  - Gorakhpur (0.2 Mn ton) starts "any time in September", with full ramp-up in Q3 (p.6).
  - Siliguri (0.3 Mn ton) starts in Q4 (p.14).
  - New Malur is now "almost a 1-million-ton plant". The CMD calls it "totally value-added", with EBITDA of "8,000 plus" (p.4, p.9), coming "by December '27" (p.10).
  - A 0.5 Mn ton western plant moves from Bhuj to the Pune corridor, and the Mumbai plant shifts entirely to exports (p.14–15).
  - The CSO says the 2 Mn ton comes "over the next two and a half years" (p.4).
- **Group Shared Services Company:** HR, IT and branding will be shared across "SG Finserv, SG Mart and APL Apollo as well as Apollo Pipes" to reduce cost (p.15). See [[group-shared-services-company]].
- **Dubai:** volume fell to 5,000–6,000 ton a month. About 70,000 ton is in transit, and there were pricing hits and demurrage. July was 10,000–12,000 ton, with 24,000–25,000 ton targeted by September (p.5–6).

## 5. What changed since the previous source of this type

Compared with [[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-concall]]:
- **The Q1 volume target was missed.** The May call pointed to ~8.75 lakh ton; the outcome was 7.45 lakh. Management did not refer back to the 8.75 figure.
- **Volume tone is firmer again:** "I don't think I will have any problem to cross 15%". In May it was "20% is difficult … targeting 15%".
- **Margin numbers restated lower:**
  - The January 2025 price increase is now "almost INR500 per ton" (p.13), against "almost INR1,500 per ton" in May (Q4C p.15).
  - SG Premium EBITDA is now ₹0–1,000/ton, against ₹1,500–2,000/ton in January ([[src-2026-01-q3-fy26-concall]], p.7).
- **The ₹500 cr liability, buyback and higher dividend were not mentioned.** Neither was the January promise of near-zero interest cost.
- **Capacity:**
  - The CSO's "two and a half years" matches the CMD's May "2 and 2.5 years", i.e. FY29, against the deck's "By FY28".
  - The CMD refers to a "7-million-ton capex plan" (p.10).
  - Malur has grown from 0.6 to ~1 Mn ton.
  - Bhuj is dropped for a Pune-corridor plant.
- **New:** the Group Shared Services Company with promoter-group firms, the end of product-line margin disclosure, solar products, and the PEB market share claim.
