# Source: Q1 FY27 earnings presentation and press release

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** earnings presentation plus press release, filed under SEBI LODR Reg. 30.
- **Date:** 1 August 2026. **Period:** Q1 FY27 (April–June 2026), with quarterly history from Q1 FY25.
- **File:** `raw/presentations/2026-08-q1-fy27-investor-presentation.pdf` (39 PDF pages, including the covering letter and press release). Page numbers below are PDF pages; the printed slide number is one lower.
- **Quality note:**
  - The P&L (p.19) and balance sheet and cash flow (p.20) tables extract with their rows misaligned. I rebuilt them by checking sums: EBITDA − interest − depreciation + other income − tax = PAT, and the operating cash flow build-up.
  - Line items marked "≈" in the tables below could not be fully pinned down.
  - **Medium confidence** for the balance sheet; high for the headline P&L.

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Volume fell, margin held.**
  - Volume: 744,823 ton (−6% YoY, −19% QoQ).
  - Revenue: ₹56.1 Bn (+8% YoY, −11% QoQ).
  - EBITDA: ₹4.1 Bn (+11% YoY, −20% QoQ).
  - EBITDA/ton: ₹5,522 (+18% YoY, flat QoQ).
  - PAT: ₹2.6 Bn (+11% YoY, −26% QoQ) (p.7).
- **Product-line EBITDA/ton is no longer shown.** The six-line product table (General, Light, Rust-proof, Coated, Heavy, Galv) is replaced by volumes in four new categories, restated back to FY24: APL Apollo brand, SG Premium brand, UAE operations and Roofing products (p.12). Management's reason is on the call ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.17).
- **Every new category except Roofing fell QoQ:**
  - SG Premium 90,504 → 58,686 ton.
  - UAE 49,835 → 25,929 ton.
  - APL Apollo brand 697,094 → 568,691 ton.
  - Roofing 87,447 → 91,516 ton (p.12).
- **Cash went backwards.**
  - Operating cash flow −₹0.3 Bn, mainly from a ₹4.6 Bn inventory build.
  - Capex ₹2.2 Bn; free cash flow −₹2.9 Bn.
  - Net cash fell from ₹15.3 Bn to ₹14.1 Bn (p.17–18, p.20).
  - The note says ₹11 Bn of fixed deposits are again counted as cash (p.20).
- **Interest cost went up, not down:** ₹389 Mn, +17% YoY and +23% QoQ (p.7, p.19).
- **Annualised returns:** ROCE 32.0% (FY26: 37.3%); ROE 19.4% (FY26: 25.3%); net working capital 0 days (p.7, p.16).
- **The capacity slide still says "Capacity Expansion Plan By FY28":** 5 Mn ton existing + 2 Mn ton greenfield/brownfield (East: Gorakhpur, Siliguri; South: Malur; Western coastal area) + 1 Mn ton debottlenecking = 8 Mn ton. It gives no plant tonnages or capex (p.10).
- **Press release:** the CMD says volume was "below our own expectations" because of the geopolitical situation, and says he expects "2HFY27 to perform much better than 1HFY27" because of higher infrastructure budget allocation (p.37).
- **Solar opportunity (new slide):** an annual APL Apollo product market of ~830,000 ton by 2030, from 33.3 GW of annual solar installations (p.24).
