# Source: Q1 FY27 unaudited results and Board meeting outcome

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** stock-exchange filing (SEBI LODR Reg. 30/33). It contains the outcome of the 1 August 2026 Board meeting, the unaudited standalone and consolidated results for the quarter ended 30 June 2026, and limited review reports by [[walker-chandiok]].
- **Date:** 1 August 2026. **Period:** Q1 FY27 (April–June 2026).
- **File:** `raw/filings/2026-08-q1-fy27-results.pdf` (13 PDF pages; page 13 is blank). Page numbers below are PDF pages.
- **Quality note:** the results tables (p.7–8, p.11–12) are scanned, and the extracted columns are scrambled. Where a figure could not be tied to a column, I took it from the earnings presentation and checked that the two reconcile ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-presentation]], p.19). **Medium confidence.**

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Blue Ocean SPA signed** for ₹160.00 cr, against a carrying amount of ₹150.30 cr; held for sale at 30 June; no gain recognised yet (p.8, note 4; p.12, note 4). Completed in Q2 (see [[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]]).

- **Limited review is unmodified.** One subsidiary was reviewed by another auditor. Its Q1 figures, as read from the scanned page, are revenue ₹289.01 cr, PAT ₹7.62 cr and total comprehensive income ₹1.62 cr (p.5). **Corrected 2026-10-28:** the Q2 filing gives this subsidiary's H1 PAT as ₹28.48 cr and Q2 PAT as ₹26.86 cr, which implies Q1 PAT of **₹1.62 cr** ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.7). The "7.62" is probably an OCR misreading of "1.62". It is the UAE subsidiary by elimination, as in Q3 FY26 (our inference). See [[apl-apollo-tubes-company-llc]].
- **New associate:** the review covers an associate, *APL Apollo Foundation*. Its share of profit is nil and its results were not reviewed (p.5–6). The annual report's entity list did not have an associate (our observation). The filing says it is not material.
- **Group Shared Services Company (SSC):** the Board approved APL Apollo's participation in a new SSC. APL Apollo will take up to 20% of its equity, for no more than ₹1 crore. The SSC will be an associate and will provide "common corporate support services to participating Group entities" (p.1). On the call, the CMD named these entities as SG Finserv, SG Mart, APL Apollo and Apollo Pipes ([[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]], p.15). See [[group-shared-services-company]].
- **Apollo Metalex plant consolidation:** the Board noted [[apollo-metalex]]'s approval to move production from its **A-25 unit at Sikandrabad** in phases to other group plants, and to dispose of the A-25 land and building. The filing says there will be no adverse impact on capacity or volumes (p.2).
- **Consolidated Q1 FY27 (₹ crore):**
  - Revenue from operations 5,606.71; PAT about 263 (basic EPS ₹9.48).
  - Net worth 5,579.01 (31 Mar 2026: 5,296.53).
  - Debt-equity (0.25)×, i.e. net cash (p.7–8).
- **Standalone Q1 FY27 (₹ crore):** revenue 3,801.88; PAT 146.98 (EPS ₹5.29); net worth 3,638.01 (p.11–12).
- The Q4 FY26 column is the audited full year less the nine-month figures (p.8, note 2). The Q1 FY26 comparatives were reviewed by Deloitte (p.5, p.10).

## 5. What changed since the previous source of this type

Compared with [[src-2026-05-q4-fy26-results]]:
- The Q4 FY26 filing's restructuring was about exits ([[blue-ocean-projects]] divestment, [[apl-apollo-mart]] liquidation). This filing adds a plant closure with a land sale, and a new **promoter-group shared-services vehicle**.
- An associate (APL Apollo Foundation) now appears in the consolidation scope.
- **Blue Ocean sale agreed (note 4).** A share purchase agreement was signed in Q1: ₹160.00 cr consideration against a ₹150.30 cr carrying amount. Consideration was received after 30 June, and the assets were classified as held for sale at 30 June (p.8, p.12). *Corrected 2026-10-28:* an earlier version of this page said there was no update; the note was scrambled in the first text extraction. There was no update on the AAML liquidation.
