# Source: Axis Securities — Q2 FY27 result update

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** sell-side broker result update by Axis Securities (analysts Manisha Kesari and Keval Barot). **Broker opinion, not company data.**
- **Date:** 27 October 2026; CMP taken as of 26 October 2026. **Period:** Q2 FY27, with estimates for FY27E–FY28E.
- **File:** `raw/Broker reports/APL Apollo Tubes - Axis Securities - Results Review - 2026-10-27.pdf` (10 PDF pages; p.7–8 are disclaimers, p.9 an advert). Page numbers below are PDF pages.
- **Quality note:** the side panels (shareholding, financial summary) extract scrambled. Figures used below are those that read cleanly or reconcile.

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Rating BUY; target price ₹2,500** (earlier ₹2,250), at 35× FY28E EPS (unchanged multiple). CMP ₹2,078, implying 20% upside; market cap ₹57,695 cr (p.1).
- **Scorecard:** revenue a marginal miss (−4% against Axis, −2% against consensus); EBITDA and PAT beat. EBITDA ₹578 cr against an Axis estimate of ₹536 cr; PAT ₹382 cr against ₹363 cr (p.1, p.3).
- **Revenue basis:** Axis reports Q2 "Consolidated revenue" as ₹6,797 cr (+25% YoY). That is sale of products, not total revenue from operations of ₹7,001.70 cr ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.9).
- **Estimates changed:**
  - FY27E: sales +1%, EBITDA −4% (₹2,289 → ₹2,203 cr), PAT −3%.
  - FY28E: sales +7%, EBITDA +8% (₹2,611 → ₹2,814 cr), PAT +8% (₹1,834 → ₹1,985 cr).
  - **Volume cut 9% (FY27E 4,200 → 3,838 kt) and 3% (FY28E 4,800 → 4,600 kt)** (p.3).
- **Axis's call summary** (p.2):
  - Management "refrained from providing a firm consolidated volume outlook".
  - FY27 EBITDA "~Rs 2,200 Cr or more".
  - UAE at ~40% of normal levels.
  - A "potential Rs 500 Cr EBITDA opportunity" from the UAE, conditional on recovery.
  - FY28 EBITDA of ~₹3,000 cr "contingent … rather than firm guidance".
  - Freight costs down ~₹150/ton.
- **Strategy as Axis describes it:** "reducing exposure to secondary-steel and price-sensitive product categories over the next 1-2 years" (p.1). Core APL Apollo brand target "15-20% YoY growth" (p.1). Capacity "fully on track" to ~8 MTPA by FY28; 10 MTPA by FY30 (p.1).
- **Errors in the note:** roofing capacity of ~0.7 MTPA is described as "a quarterly volume opportunity of ~75KT at full utilisation" (p.2); 0.7 MTPA a year is ~175 kt a quarter (our calc.). Roofing end-markets are given as housing 50% / industrial-commercial 50% on p.1, but 50/25/25 on the call.
- **Key risks named:** delay in UAE recovery and expansion ramp-up; a steep HRC fall causing trader destocking; delay in capacity execution (p.2).
