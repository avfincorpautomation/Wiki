## 2. Key takeaways

- **The company-wide volume guidance is effectively withdrawn.**
  - Asked for revised FY27 volume guidance, the CMD kept 15% growth only for "our profitable business, like the APL Apollo brand and roofing products".
  - For SG Premium and the UAE: "we are not in a position to commit any volume growth – we don't know right now" (06:31). Also: "on UAE and SG Premium, I cannot say" (28:32).
- **EBITDA is the target.**
  - CSO: "on track to achieve 20% growth in absolute EBITDA for FY27" (01:16).
  - CMD: FY27 EBITDA ~₹2,200 cr, FY28 ~₹2,500 cr (30:27; reconstructed).
  - CMD: "5 million tonnes at Rs 6,000 per tonne – that is Rs 3,000 crore of EBITDA … it will come", conditional on Dubai and domestic steel availability (32:51–35:56).
- **EBITDA/ton:**
  - CSO: ₹6,000 "is now sustainable" (01:16).
  - CMD: "Rs 6,000-plus in Q3" and "Q3 is much better than this" (26:07).
  - Management says it will revise guidance after two quarters at ₹6,000+ (38:51–40:20).
- **Source of the margin gain:**
  - The CSO concedes **gross profit per ton fell ~₹500 QoQ**, because light structures (lower realisation) are now 25% of the APL Apollo brand mix, against the mid-to-high teens before (21:45–22:38). The EBITDA/ton rise therefore came from operating leverage and cost cuts.
  - The CMD cites exiting "non-profitable business", added roofing capacity, and cost cuts in freight, power (₹6.2–6.3/unit against a ₹5 target) and logistics (13:07, 17:01, 37:24).
- **Value-added redefined:** "Barring SG Premium, everything is value-added" (50:18). The APL Apollo brand and Roofing together are "almost 92–93% of total volume" (43:55).
- **Steel shortage:** "there is no steel … HR availability is bad. The plants will be back on track from December" (34:25). Asked whether lower volume was a choice, the CMD said no.
- **Cash:**
  - CSO: H1 operating cash flow ~₹700 cr, capex ~₹400 cr (against ₹600 cr in all of FY26), free cash flow ~₹200 cr. The dividend was fully paid in H1.
  - For H2 he describes "around Rs 1,200 crore of EBITDA, minus taxes, with zero dividend outflow and hardly Rs 200 crore towards capex", and says "we will surpass the Rs 1,300 crore of free cash flow we generated for the whole of last year" (17:40–20:22).
- **Dubai:** capacity to rise from ~0.3 to **0.5 Mn ton by March 2027** (two new mills), with EBITDA of "Rs 8,000–10,000 per tonne" (41:52). The current run rate is ~100,000 ton a year (45:33). CMD: "it will be ready as a Rs 10,000-per-tonne EBITDA business, but right now it is out of my hands" (29:36).
- **New products:** a specialised mill making up to 250×250 mm, 16 mm, is seeding defence and aerospace products. Samples are with "various authorities", with results expected "in the next two to three months" (01:16). Items developed for ISRO and DRDO (37:24).
- **Other:**
  - Roofing capacity is 50–60 thousand ton a month (~7 lakh ton a year) (11:14), with 1,75,000 ton targeted (23:24).
  - The FDs over one year were reclassified to current (08:37).
  - The CSO declined to give the heavy/super-heavy split: "We have stopped giving that sensitive information" (42:37).

## 5. What changed since the previous source of this type

Compared with [[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-concall]]:
- **Volume guidance narrowed.** In August: company-wide 15–20%, 15% "confidently", Q2 10 / Q3 10.5 / Q4 12 lakh ton. Now: only the brand and Roofing are committed, and no quarterly path is given.
- **EBITDA/ton range raised:** from ₹5,000–5,500 "throughout the year" to ₹6,000 "sustainable" and "6,000-plus in Q3".
- **Dubai EBITDA:** from ₹5,000–6,000/ton "if operations ramp up" (August) to ₹8,000–10,000 with the new mills (October).
- **Capacity:** "next two years" for 8 Mn ton (October), against "two and a half years" (August). Gorakhpur, Siliguri and Malur are not mentioned at all.
- **Not discussed:** the ₹500 cr liability, buyback or dividend increase, the jump in gross debt, the SSC acquisition, and the Blue Ocean buyer.
