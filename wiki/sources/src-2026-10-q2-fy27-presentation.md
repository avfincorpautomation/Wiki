# Source: Q2 FY27 earnings presentation and press release

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** earnings presentation ("The Architecture of Returns") plus press release, filed under SEBI LODR Reg. 30.
- **Date:** 26 October 2026. **Period:** Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27, with quarterly history from Q1 FY25.
- **File:** `raw/presentations/2026-10-q2-fy27-investor-presentation.pdf` (38 PDF pages, including the covering letter and press release). Page numbers below are PDF pages; the printed slide number is one lower.
- **Quality note:** the volume table and quarterly snapshot (p.9, p.13) extract cleanly. The P&L (p.19) and balance sheet and cash flow (p.20) tables are misaligned and were rebuilt by checking sums. Medium confidence on those two slides.

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Record quarter.**
  - Volume 963,143 ton (+13% YoY, +29% QoQ); revenue ₹70.0 Bn.
  - EBITDA ₹5.8 Bn (+29% YoY, +40% QoQ); EBITDA/ton **₹5,999** (+15% YoY, +9% QoQ).
  - PAT ₹3.8 Bn (+27% YoY) (p.7).
- **H1 FY27:** volume 1,708 thousand ton (+4%); EBITDA ₹9.9 Bn (+21%); EBITDA/ton ₹5,791; PAT ₹6.5 Bn (+20%) (p.8).
- **The revenue growth figures overstate the filing.**
  - The presentation shows +34% YoY for Q2 and +22% for H1 (p.7–8).
  - The presentation's Q2 FY26 revenue (₹52,063 Mn) excludes the ₹6.19 Bn of traded sales regrouped into FY26 quarters. The filed Q2 FY26 figure is ₹5,531.28 cr.
  - On filed numbers, growth is +26.6% (Q2) and +17.8% (H1) (our calculation; [[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.9).
- **Volume by category:** APL Apollo brand 774,751 ton (+19% YoY); Roofing 135,255 (+29%); SG Premium 26,910 (−54% QoQ); UAE 26,227 (−55% YoY) (p.13).
- **Interest cost ₹524 Mn** (+90% YoY, +35% QoQ) (p.7).
- **Net cash ₹16.3 Bn** (FY26 ₹15.3 Bn). This counts ₹14 Bn of fixed deposits as cash. Implied gross debt is ₹9,181 Mn (₹25,462 Mn cash & bank less ₹16,281 Mn net cash), against ₹4,525 Mn at FY26 (p.18, p.20; our calculation).
- **H1 cash flow:**
  - Operating cash flow ₹6,827 Mn; capex ₹4,091 Mn; free cash flow ₹1,795 Mn.
  - Dividend ₹2,360 Mn; "others" +₹1,523 Mn, probably mostly the Blue Ocean proceeds (our inference) (p.18, p.20).
- **Annualised H1 returns:** ROCE 39.7%, ROE 27.7%; net working capital 0 days (p.7–8, p.16).
- **Capacity:** the section is headed "CAPEX PLAN ON TRACK", with the unchanged "Capacity Expansion Plan By FY28" slide (5 + 2 + 1 = 8 Mn ton, no plant detail) (p.10–11).
- **Typo:** H1 FY27 EBITDA/ton is printed as 11,521 in the P&L table (p.19); it should be 5,791 (p.8). Management acknowledged this on the call ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 27:44).
- **ESG:**
  - The headline target now matches the annual report: Scope 1 and 2 −58.8% by FY2034 against FY2024; net zero by 2050; renewable electricity above 50% (p.27–28). The timeline slide still carries the old "25% by 2030" wording.
  - FY26 renewable electricity 58%; worker LTIFR 1.9 (FY25 10.8); 7 of 11 plants ZLD-compliant (p.29).
- **Press release:** "strongest-ever quarterly performance", with EBITDA/ton "supported by a higher share of value-added products and the strength of the APL Apollo brand". It expects "2HFY27 to perform better than 1HFY27" (p.36).

## 5. What changed since the previous source of this type

Compared with [[src-2026-08-q1-fy27-presentation]]:
- **Margin up, cash flow positive.** H1 operating cash flow is ₹6.8 Bn, against −₹0.3 Bn in Q1, as inventory partly unwound.
- **Debt doubled** (implied ₹4.3 → ₹9.2 Bn), while reported net cash rose. The cash and FD pile and the borrowings both grew.
- **The press release wording changed.** August said H2 would be "much better than" H1; October says "better than".
- **The ESG slides now show the 58.8% target**, partly resolving C5.
