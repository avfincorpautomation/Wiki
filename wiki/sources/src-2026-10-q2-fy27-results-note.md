# Source: Q2 FY27 results note and concall summary (unattributed)

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** a results note with a financial snapshot, valuation table and concall summary. **No author or firm is named.** It refers to a "broker estimate" (p.1), so it is probably a broker or research-service note, origin unknown.
- **Date:** headed 26 October 2026, 12:25 pm. The valuation table is dated 27 October 2026 (p.1–2). **Period:** Q2 FY27 and H1 FY27.
- **File:** `raw/Broker reports/2026-10-q2-fy27-results-note.pdf` (8 PDF pages; p.8 is blank). Page numbers below are PDF pages.
- **Use:** its financial snapshot matches the filed results, which is a useful cross-check. Its concall summary includes several points missing from the unofficial machine transcript ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]]). Because the author is unknown, **treat those concall points as medium confidence.**

## 2. Key takeaways

- **The snapshot ties to the filing:** revenue ₹7,001.6 cr (+26.6% YoY), EBITDA ₹577.7 cr (+29.3%), PAT ₹382.3 cr (+26.8%). H1 revenue +17.8%, EBITDA +20.8% (p.1). This uses the filed basis, not the presentation's +34%.
- **Balance sheet (H1 vs FY26):** **borrowings ₹452.5 cr → ₹918.1 cr (+102.9%)**; net worth ₹5,296.5 → ₹5,740.3 cr; trade payables ₹2,368.3 → ₹2,565.3 cr; cash equivalents ₹886.2 → ₹1,149.8 cr (p.1). This independently confirms the debt figure in [[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]].
- **H1 cash flow** uses the statutory figures: operating cash flow ₹593.9 cr; capex ₹409.1 cr; free cash flow ₹184.8 cr (p.1).
- **Against the note's own estimates:** revenue −3.0% (₹7,219 cr expected); EBITDA ₹577.7 cr against ₹536 cr; PAT ₹382.3 cr against ₹360 cr (p.1).
- **Market data, 27 October 2026:**
  - Price ₹2,214; market cap ₹61,438.5 cr; EV ₹61,206.8 cr; 52-week range ₹2,301 / ₹1,690.
  - **P/E TTM 46.9×; EV/EBITDA TTM 31.0×**; market cap to sales 2.5×.
  - Holdings: promoters 28.2%, FIIs 34.2%, DIIs 20.8% (p.2).
- **Concall points not in the machine transcript** (p.3–7):
  - UAE "at only around 40% of their normal operating levels".
  - "approximately 15,000 tonnes of monthly exports from India" affected by container freight.
  - UAE described as "a potential ₹500 crore EBITDA business when fully operational".
  - FY27 EBITDA "could potentially reach approximately ₹2,400 crore if the UAE business had operated normally", but this is not the base case.
  - "Possibility of increasing net cash to approximately ₹2,000 crore by the end of FY27".
  - Roofing: new production lines commissioned in July.
  - Channel discounts can swing profit by ~₹10–15 cr a quarter.
  - Power cost target of ₹5/unit "in the following year" (from ~₹7 earlier; ₹6.2–6.3 now).
- **Lower-margin products:** "The company wanted to reduce the share of this lower-margin business to approximately 10–20% over the following one to two years, with the intention of progressively moving away from the segment" (p.7). This **contradicts** the machine transcript's version, in which the CMD aims to *take* 10–20% share of the secondary-steel segment. Axis agrees with this note ([[src-2026-10-axis-q2-fy27-note]], p.1). See C32 in [[open-questions]].
- **Core-category target:** "approximately 15–20%" YoY growth for the APL Apollo brand and Roofing (p.3), against "15%" in the transcript.
