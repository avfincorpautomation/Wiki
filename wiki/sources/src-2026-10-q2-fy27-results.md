# Source: Q2 FY27 unaudited results and Board meeting outcome

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** stock-exchange filing (SEBI LODR Reg. 30/33). It contains:
  - the outcome of the 26 October 2026 Board meeting;
  - Reg. 30 details of the SG Enterprise Solutions acquisition;
  - unaudited standalone and consolidated results, balance sheet and half-year cash flow;
  - limited review reports by [[walker-chandiok]].
- **Date:** 26 October 2026. **Period:** Q2 FY27 (July–September 2026) and H1 FY27.
- **File:** `raw/filings/2026-10-q2-fy27-results.pdf` (19 PDF pages; p.19 is blank). Page numbers below are PDF pages.
- **Quality note:**
  - The narrative pages (p.1–8, p.12, p.18) are clean text.
  - The financial tables (p.9–11, p.15–17) extract with columns misaligned. Line items used here were tied out by sums: finance cost Q1 + Q2 = H1; depreciation likewise; debt checked against the presentation.
  - **Medium confidence** on table items; high on the notes.

## 2. Key takeaways

- **SG Enterprise Solutions becomes a subsidiary.** The Board approved buying a further 31% (15,500 shares of ₹10) of SG Enterprise Solutions Pvt Ltd, the Group Shared Services Company, for ₹1,55,000. This takes APL Apollo from 20% to 51%.
  - The sellers are **S Gupta Holding Pvt Ltd (7,750 shares)** and **SG Green Buildings Pvt Ltd (7,750 shares)**. The filing calls both related parties: S Gupta Holding is promoter group, and the promoters and their relatives hold more than 20% of SG Green Buildings.
  - The SSC was incorporated on 10 September 2026, has not started operations, and has 50,000 shares in issue.
  - Completion is expected by 31 December 2026, "on an arm's length basis" (p.1, p.3–5). See [[group-shared-services-company]].
- **Blue Ocean sale completed.** The share purchase agreement was signed in Q1, for ₹160.00 cr against a carrying amount of ₹150.30 cr, and completed in Q2.
  - De-recognised: PPE ₹97.78 cr, right-of-use assets ₹21.50 cr and net current assets ₹31.02 cr.
  - **Gain of ₹9.70 cr in other income** (p.12, note 3).
  - The buyer is not named. See [[blue-ocean-projects]].
- **APL Apollo Mart liquidation completed.** APL Apollo **received ₹109.36 cr** (standalone note 4, p.18). A dissolution application is pending before the NCLT, New Delhi.
  - The consolidated note dates completion to "the quarter ended September 30, 2025" (p.12, note 5). That is apparently a typo for 2026. See [[apl-apollo-mart]].
- **A-25 Sikandrabad.** [[apollo-metalex]] has signed an agreement to sell the land and building to a third party. Completion conditions were not met at quarter-end, so **₹15.59 cr is held for sale** and no gain is recognised (p.12, note 4).
- **Consolidated Q2 FY27 (₹ crore):**
  - Revenue 7,001.70 (Q1 5,606.71; Q2 FY26 5,531.28), i.e. **+26.6% YoY** (our calculation).
  - Other income 40.16; PBT 499.70; tax 117.28; PAT 382.42; basic EPS ₹13.62 (p.9).
- **Gross debt roughly doubled.**
  - Non-current borrowings ≈187.80 (31 Mar 260.52); current borrowings ≈730.31 (191.95). Total ≈₹918 cr against ₹452 cr.
  - The cash flow shows net current borrowings of +₹538.36 cr in H1.
  - Finance costs ₹52.40 cr in Q2, against ₹38.94 cr in Q1 and ₹27.60 cr in Q2 FY26 (p.9–11).
- **Statutory H1 operating cash flow ₹593.89 cr** (H1 FY26 ₹600.05 cr). Capex including capital advances ≈₹425 cr. Dividend paid ₹236.01 cr (p.11).
- **Scope of consolidation:** two associates now (APL Apollo Foundation and SG Enterprise Solutions). The UAE subsidiary is reviewed by another auditor:
  - total assets ₹1,090.93 cr;
  - revenue Q2 ₹425.01 cr, H1 ₹714.02 cr;
  - PAT Q2 ₹26.86 cr, H1 ₹28.48 cr (p.7–8).
