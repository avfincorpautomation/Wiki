# Source: Systematix Institutional Equities — Q2 FY27 result update

## 1. What it is

- **Type:** sell-side broker result update by Systematix Shares and Stocks (analysts Shweta Dikshit and Vaibhav Somani). **Broker opinion, not company data.**
- **Date:** 26 October 2026. **Period:** Q2 FY27, with estimates for FY27E–FY29E.
- **File:** `raw/Broker reports/APL Apollo Tubes - Systematix - Initiating Coverage - 2026-10-26.pdf` (8 PDF pages; p.5–6 are disclosures, p.7 an advert). Page numbers below are PDF pages.
- **Naming note:** the file name says "Initiating Coverage", but the report is headed "RESULT UPDATE" and says "We maintain BUY" (p.1). It is a result update.
- **Quality note:** the financial tables on p.4 extract scrambled. Figures below come from the clearly readable text on p.1–3.

## 2. Key takeaways

- **Rating BUY; target price ₹2,517**, at 25× EV/EBITDA for the 12 months to September 2028. CMP ₹2,078, implying 21% upside; market cap ₹577 bn (p.1–2).
- **Scorecard:** EBITDA (excl. other income) ₹5.8 bn, **7.6% above** Systematix's estimate. Revenue ₹70.1 bn, **5.3% below**. Systematix quotes revenue growth of +34.5% YoY, the presentation basis (p.1). See C29 in [[open-questions]].
- **Net cash on a strict basis:** "APAT closed 1HFY27 with a net cash position of **Rs 2.3bn** (versus Rs 4.3bn net cash as of FY26)" (p.1). This leaves out the ~₹14 Bn of fixed deposits that the company counts as cash. Our check against the filing: cash ₹892.08 cr + other bank balances ₹257.71 cr − borrowings ≈₹918 cr = ₹232 cr ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-results]], p.10). That matches ₹2.3 bn.
- **Estimates:**
  - Volume 3.7 / 4.4 / 5.0 Mt for FY27E / FY28E / FY29E.
  - EBITDA/ton ₹5,958 / ₹5,968 / ₹6,002.
  - EBITDA ₹22 / 26 / 30 bn; PAT ₹15 / 18 / 21 bn; EPS ₹54 / 64 / 74.
  - FY27E/FY28E EBITDA raised 6% / 4% (p.1–2).
- **Management highlights as reported** (p.1, p.3):
  - H1 capex ₹4 bn, with a further ₹2 bn planned in H2.
  - H2 EBITDA target ₹12 bn.
  - 15% volume growth in the brand and Roofing in H2, "in line with its total volume guidance"; UAE growth contingent on the Middle East.
  - EBITDA/ton above ₹6,000 in Q3.
  - "Roofing capacity of ~0.7mtpa, with quarterly production potential of around 175kt at full capacity".
- Systematix's analyst (Shweta Dikshit) asked the margin-sustainability question on the call ([[src-2026-10-q2-fy27-concall]], 36:27).
